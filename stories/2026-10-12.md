# Toby’s Cozy Little Goal

**Date:** October 12, 2026  
**Theme:** setting a goal and working toward it  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Once, on a chilly October evening, a little bear named Toby sat by the window. The autumn wind blew red and gold leaves through the yard. Toby had a big idea. He wanted to make the coziest reading fort his room had ever seen.

"Every big project starts with a goal," Mama Bear said gently. "You can write it down so you remember each step."

Toby smiled. He found a blue crayon to write his plan: *Step one, find blankets. Step two, fix the roof. Step three, add pillows.*

"May I help you?" asked his little sister, Ruby.

"Yes, we can do it together!" Toby cheered. 

First, they needed warm quilts. Toby opened the wooden trunk. "Look at those fluffy ones," he whispered. He picked them up with two strong paws. Mama helped wash the dusty quilt so it smelled like sweet lavender, and Toby helped fold it with care. 

Step by step, the fort began to grow. Toby asked, "Does this blanket reach the chair?" Ruby pulled one corner, but it slipped with a silly swoosh, making them both laugh. Toby did not give up. He tried again, clipping the edges tightly until the roof stayed firm.

"See?" Toby beamed proudly. "When we take small steps, hard things become easy."

For the final step, they put three soft cushions inside and turned on a warm little lantern. It was the best fort in the whole forest.

Outside, the autumn moon rose high into the starry sky. Toby, Ruby, and Mama crawled inside the snug shelter. Toby’s eyes felt very heavy. He had set a goal, worked hard, and finished what he started. 

"Time to lay down and rest," Mama whispered softly. 

Toby curled under the warm quilt, listened to the quiet breeze outside, and drifted happily to sleep.
