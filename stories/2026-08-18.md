# The Sweet Berry Feast

**Date:** August 18, 2026  
**Theme:** sharing with friends  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Mia was a very small field mouse with soft gray fur. She always loved warm August evenings because the forest berries were sweet and ripe. 

Tonight, Mia found a hidden bush full of shiny purple berries. They were the biggest berries she had ever seen! Her tummy gave a tiny rumble. For a moment, Mia thought about keeping the whole treat for herself. She could run fast to her cozy burrow and hide. But no, that did not feel quite right to her. 

Just then, Mia saw her friend Toby the turtle resting near the path. Toby looked tired after a long day of exploring. Mia walked over and gave the sweetest berries to him. 

Toby’s eyes lit up with delight. "Thank you, Mia!" he whispered. He did a slow, happy little dance right on the moss. 

Mia felt a warm glow inside her chest. She realized that sharing was the best part of finding something special. It was wonderful because seeing Toby smile made her smile, too. 

The two friends sat side by side, enjoying the delicious fruit together. When they finished, Toby helped Mia wash their sticky paws in the cool water of the brook. 

The warm sun began to sink below the hills. The forest grew quiet and still before the silver stars came out to twinkle. A sleepy songbird flew gently to its nest high in the leafy branches, tucking its wings in tight. 

"Good night, Mia," Toby said, letting out a big, cozy yawn. 

"Good night, Toby," she whispered back. 

Mia curled up safely inside her soft grass bed. Her belly was full, and her heart felt peaceful and light. The gentle breeze blew softly through the trees, and Mia closed her eyes, drifting into sweet dreams.
