# The Secret of the Silver Moon Room

**Date:** May 10, 2026  
**Theme:** keeping your room tidy  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Leo loved to play in his bedroom, but tonight the floor looked like a giant mountain of colorful toys. "It is **time** for bed," his mom said as she poked her head inside the door. She **gave** him a warm, cozy smile, but Leo looked **down** at the floor and sighed. He could not even see his rug! Worst of all, he could not find his favorite stuffed owl, Hoot.

"I have **been** playing all day," Leo said. "Now the room is so messy that I do not know where to **start**. There are blocks and cars everywhere!"

Mom sat on the edge of the bed. "Let me **tell** you a secret," she said quietly. "**Once** there was a **small** mouse who lived in a very messy nest. He could never find his snacks! But when he put **each** tiny twig in its place, he had more room to jump and dance."

Leo laughed and decided to try it. He began to **walk** over to his big wooden toy box. He picked up his shiny race cars first. He did not need to **buy** more toys; he just needed to find the ones he already had! He put the cars away, **so** the blue rug began to show again. It felt nice to see the floor.

Next, Leo found his puzzle pieces. He put them in their yellow box. "Is **my** owl in his house yet?" Leo asked. He looked under a soft pile of pillows. There he was! Hoot was waiting for a bedtime hug. Leo felt very proud. The room felt peaceful and wide now, not crowded or loud.

Leo tucked Hoot under his arm. He liked how his room felt when it was tidy. It was like a giant, soft hug for his brain. Mom turned the **light** low until the room was soft and golden. 

"Now it is easy to dream," Mom whispered. Leo climbed into his bed and pulled up his fuzzy blanket. It was finally time to **sleep**. He closed his eyes, feeling happy and calm in his neat, quiet room.
