# The Song of the Silver Coins

**Date:** July 11, 2026  
**Theme:** saving money and piggy banks  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Leo had a little green piggy bank named Pip. Pip sat on the shelf by the window, catching the warm summer light of July. Leo loved to look at Pip every day. He wanted to buy a shiny blue toy star to hang from his ceiling. 

"To get the star, you must save your coins," his mother said with a smile.

"I know!" Leo said. "I can save a little bit at a time."

Leo did small chores to earn shiny silver quarters. He helped wash the dog and sweep the kitchen floor. Every time he got a coin, he ran to Pip. *Clink, clank!* went the coins as they dropped inside.

"I like that sound," Leo whispered. "It is so sweet."

"Let us see how many we have," said Dad. 

They poured the quarters onto the soft bed. There were six shiny coins. Leo wanted to count them slowly. He did not want to spend them fast. He wanted to save even more so his little green friend would feel heavy and full. 

Leo used a blue crayon to write his name on a small card. He drew a picture of the shiny star on the card, too. He taped it to Pip’s belly. Now, he could think about his dream every time he saved a new coin. 

"You are doing a great job," Dad said, tucking him in. "Soon, you will have enough."

Leo smiled. He carefully put all the quarters back inside Pip. *Clink! Clink!* Pip seemed to sing a gentle, quiet lullaby as Leo closed his eyes. 

"Goodnight, my shiny star," Leo whispered. "Goodnight, Pip."

He drifted off to sleep, feeling happy and safe, knowing his savings were growing warm and secure through the cozy summer night.
