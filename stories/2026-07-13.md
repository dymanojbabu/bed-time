# Mia’s Honest Heart

**Date:** July 13, 2026  
**Theme:** being honest even when it is hard  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The summer night was warm, **but** Mia felt **very** **cold** inside. She stood in the hallway and stared at the wooden floor. A pretty blue bowl lay broken in the shadow. It was **her** grandmother’s favorite bowl, the special one **that** **has** a **number** of tiny painted stars on the side. Mia had climbed **up** on a chair to **get** her favorite bedtime book. **But** her elbow had bumped the shelf, and the bowl fell. Oh, **how** she wished she could turn back time!

Mia wanted to hide the broken pieces under the rug. She did not want **her** grandmother to be sad. **When** we make mistakes, it can be **very** hard to tell the truth. Mia took a deep, quiet breath. She knew she had to be brave. She walked into the kitchen where Grandma was sitting by the soft yellow **light** of the lamp, waiting to **read** a story.

"Grandma," Mia said, **her** voice **very** soft. "I made a mistake. I broke the blue bowl when I tried to reach my book." Mia looked down, waiting. **But** Grandma did not **look** angry. She knelt down and gave Mia a big, warm hug. "Thank you for being honest, Mia," Grandma whispered. "A bowl can be glued back together, **but** truth is a treasure **that** we must always keep safe."

Grandma helped Mia sweep **up** the pieces. "**Look** at **those** shiny stars," Grandma said, pointing to the blue clay. "We will make a new game out of fixing it tomorrow." Mia smiled. The heavy, **cold** feeling in her tummy was completely gone. She climbed into her cozy bed, thinking about **how** good it felt to tell the truth. Under the soft starlight, she closed her eyes and drifted off to a happy, peaceful sleep.
