# The Thank You Blanket

**Date:** August 20, 2026  
**Theme:** gratitude and saying thank you  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The sun began to dip low behind the tall oak trees, painting the sky in soft shades of peach and lavender. It had been a long, sunny day, and little Mia was ready to rest. Her cozy bedroom felt quiet and cool as the evening arrived. 

Mia climbed up into her soft bed. She pulled our favorite fluffy quilt up to her chin, but she was not quite asleep yet. Her eyes were wide with wonder.

"Mom," Mia whispered, "what was the best part of today?"

Mom sat down next to her on the mattress and smiled. "I loved when we did our garden work together. We planted little seeds, and the warm earth felt so nice on our hands."

Mia nodded softly. She remembered what else they had seen outside. "And we found that shiny blue feather by the fence! I want to write down a thank you note to the world for all of those sweet things."

"You can say thank you right inside your heart," Mom said gently. "Gratitude is like a warm hug you give to your day before you fall asleep."

Mia closed her eyes. In her mind, she thought of all the little things that made her smile. She whispered thank you to the golden sun that helped the daisies grow. She said thank you for the cool water she drank, and for the soft breeze that cooled her cheeks. Most of all, she was thankful for her family and her safe, warm bed. 

"Thank you, day," Mia murmured into her pillow.

Outside the window, the crickets began to sing their gentle bedtime song, making sweet music together in the grass. Saying thank you had made Mia's heart feel light, happy, and calm. She let out a long, peaceful breath and drifted off into a night of sweet, quiet dreams.
