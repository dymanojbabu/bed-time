# Oliver’s Thank-You Leaves

**Date:** October 20, 2026  
**Theme:** gratitude and saying thank you  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The autumn wind blew golden leaves outside Oliver’s window. It was a cool October night, and it had been a long day of playing in the tall woods. Now, it was time to get ready for sleep. Oliver walked into the bathroom to wash his paws. The warm water felt soft and gentle on his fur.

Mama Bear smiled as she handed him a fluffy towel. “Before we open your favorite storybook,” she whispered, “let us think about what made you happy today.”

Oliver sat down at his little wooden desk. He liked to write little notes with his favorite blue crayon. He wanted to make a list of kind things from his day, so he could say thank you for each one. 

Oliver looked up at his mother. “I am thankful for my yellow scarf,” he said softly. “It kept the cold wind away all the way to the river.”

Mama patted his back with a gentle paw. “Saying thank you always warms our hearts,” she told him. “It reminds us of all the good things around us.”

Oliver thought some more as the moon rose into the dark sky. The fun day had gone by fast, but he remembered every sweet part. He loved the sweet red apples they shared at lunch. He loved listening to the dry leaves crunch beneath his boots. Most of all, he loved being close to his family.

“Thank you, Mama, for that big bowl of warm soup tonight,” Oliver said with a quiet, sleepy yawn.

Mama kissed Oliver right between his fuzzy ears. “You are so welcome, my sweet little bear.”

Oliver placed his blue crayon down and tucked his note beside his pillow. He climbed into his soft bed and pulled the blankets up to his chin. The room was peaceful, cozy, and still. Oliver closed his eyes, his heart feeling light and thankful. As the stars twinkled outside, he drifted happily to sleep.
