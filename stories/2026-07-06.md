# Barnaby’s Moon Tower

**Date:** July 06, 2026  
**Theme:** setting a goal and working toward it  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Barnaby the little bear had a big, bright dream. He wanted to build a tiny stone tower in his backyard. He wanted to climb to the top to look at the friendly moon. "It is a big goal for a little bear," his mama said with a smile. "But we will work on it together, day by day."

First, they had to search for just the right stones. Barnaby looked around the grassy meadow. He wanted to find those flat, smooth stones that were easy to stack. The summer sun was high and warm on his fuzzy back. It was hard work to carry them, but Barnaby did not give up. Each day, he brought three new stones into the yard. 

"Come and sit under the shady tree," his mama would call out. Then, they would drink sweet berry juice and rest. Barnaby liked to count his stones and plan his next step.

Barnaby learned that to keep his tower standing high, he had to put the biggest, heaviest stones at the bottom. It was like putting together a beautiful puzzle. Some people might think a little bear would get tired of working. But Barnaby loved watching his goal grow taller and taller.

On the fifth day, the tower was finally finished. It stood strong and straight. 

That evening, the sky turned a deep, quiet purple. Barnaby climbed to the very top stone. He felt so proud. He had set a goal, worked hard, and finished it. 

"We did it," Barnaby whispered to the stars. 

His mama wrapped a cozy blanket around his shoulders. "You did it," she corrected gently. "You worked hard every day."

Barnaby smiled and let out a big, happy yawn. He climbed down into his mama's warm arms. As the silver moon shone down on his special tower, the little bear closed his eyes and drifted off to sleep, ready for tomorrow's dreams.
