# Oliver Bunny’s Sweet Surprise

**Date:** May 19, 2026  
**Theme:** trying new foods  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Oliver the little bunny loved carrots. He believed that carrots were the best food in all the forest. Every single day, his mother made him a crunchy orange lunch. But tonight was a little bit different. 

On his plate sat a little green pod. It looked like a tiny, bumpy green boat. 

"What is this?" Oliver asked, spinning his wooden plate around and around. 

"It is a sweet sugar pea," his mother smiled gently. "It grew near the garden wall under the warm sun."

Oliver felt his tummy do a little flip. He did not want to try it. He only wanted his usual, safe orange carrots. He wished he could make the green pod disappear. 

"Just take one tiny bite," his mother said. "It does not work to guess the taste before you try. You might have a wonderful surprise!"

Oliver looked closely at the pod. It was cool and bright green. He picked it up with his soft paws. He took a deep, quiet breath to start. *Crunch!*

A sweet, cool juice filled his mouth. It tasted like fresh spring rain. It was not scary at all!

"Wow," Oliver whispered, his eyes wide. He ate the rest of the pea. It did not go fast, because he wanted to taste every sweet bite. "What a wonderful treat!"

"There are a number of yummy things to try in our garden," his mother said, brushing his soft ears. "Each one is like a little adventure."

Oliver felt very happy and proud of himself. His tummy was full and warm. He washed his paws with some cold, clear water from the brook. Then, he hopped into his soft, cozy bed.

As the silver moon goes up into the starry night sky, Oliver closed his eyes. He dreamed of colorful gardens, sweet green peas, and bright orange carrots. He was ready for a peaceful sleep, and he knew that tomorrow would bring even more wonderful things to discover. Goodnight, Oliver.
