# Hazel’s Thank-You Night

**Date:** October 16, 2026  
**Theme:** gratitude and saying thank you  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The October wind blew softly through the tall trees, dancing with red and gold leaves. Little Hazel the squirrel sat on a mossy branch, ready for bed. It had been a long day of autumn play.

Hazel gathered her favorite treasures to take inside. She wanted to find a safe spot to put them before sleep. First were three smooth acorns. "Thank you, big tree, for sharing those," Hazel whispered into the quiet air. The oak tree swayed gently in the breeze, as if it did hear her sweet words.

Next, Hazel picked up her soft knitted scarf. It felt very gentle against her paws. Hazel liked to use it on chilly evenings to stay warm. "Thank you, Grandma, for knitting this scarf," she said with a smile. 

She carried her things inside her cozy tree hollow. Her mother was waiting with an open book and a little cup of warm berry tea. 

"Come sit by me," Mama said softly. "Tell me, how was your day?"

Hazel curled close and took one tiny sip of tea. "It was a happy day, Mama. Tonight, I made a wish to say thank you to all the good things around me."

Mama gently smoothed Hazel's fur. "Saying thank you is like giving a gift from your own heart," Mama whispered. "It fills you up with peace."

Hazel let out a sweet sigh. She looked around her safe little room and thought about the bright sun, the crisp leaves, and Mama’s hugs. 

"Thank you, soft nest," Hazel murmured, snuggling deep under her moss blanket. "Thank you, glowing moon."

Outside, the stars twinkled like tiny nightlights in the dark sky. The woods were quiet and still. Hazel closed her eyes, feeling thankful and loved, and drifted happily off to sleep.
