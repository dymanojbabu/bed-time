# Sam’s Little Red Sail

**Date:** May 11, 2026  
**Theme:** setting a goal and working toward it  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Sam had a very big goal. He wanted to make a small wooden boat that could float all by itself. He looked at a plain block of wood and asked, “Who can help me start this project?”

Grandpa smiled and patted the wooden bench in his shop. “We can do it together,” he said. “Sit here by me. First, we must read the book to see the right steps. Building a boat is not fast work. It takes time to do it well, but if we try our best, it will be wonderful.”

They spent the whole afternoon in the cozy shop. Sam had to sand the wood until it was very smooth. His little arms felt a bit tired, but he did not give up. He thought about how the boat would look on the blue water of the pond. Every time a part was tricky, he took a deep breath and started again. Grandpa told Sam, “When you have a goal, you just take one small step at a time. Soon, all those steps lead you to the finish line.”

Now, the sun was beginning to set behind the trees. They had both worked very hard. The boat was finally finished! It had a bright red sail and a sturdy hull. The air outside was turning cold as the stars came out, but inside the shop, Sam felt warm and proud. He looked at the boat he had built with his own hands. He had set a goal, worked hard, and finished what he started.

“Can you tell me a story about the sea now?” Sam asked as he climbed into his soft bed. Grandpa wrapped a fuzzy blanket around Sam’s shoulders. He told a quiet tale of tiny ships and friendly fish. Sam listened to the calm rhythm of Grandpa’s voice. He felt happy and sleepy after his busy day. As he closed his eyes, he imagined his little red sail bobbing gently on the water. It was a peaceful night, and Sam drifted off to sleep, ready for a new day of adventure.
