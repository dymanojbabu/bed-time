# Barnaby’s Wishing Tower

**Date:** June 11, 2026  
**Theme:** setting a goal and working toward it  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

On a warm evening in June, Barnaby the little bear sat by the quiet, blue river. He looked out at the smooth, round stones. He wanted to build a tall wishing tower. It was a big goal for a little bear, but he knew he could do it if he worked toward it day by day. 

"I have a plan," Barnaby whispered to the wind. "I will start right now."

His goal was to stack five smooth stones. He knew this would take patience. He had to learn how to balance each one just right. Barnaby found the first rock. It was flat and gray. He placed it down with care. That was number one.

Next, he searched for a second stone which was a bit smaller. He placed it on top. Oh, no! It tumbled down. Barnaby did not get upset. He took a deep breath. He tried again, slowly. This time, the stone stayed. 

Some forest people, like the busy little birds and squirrels, stopped to watch him. They cheered as Barnaby found his third and fourth stones. He was so happy! The river goes *swish, swish* as if it were singing a soft song.

Now, he needed just one more stone to finish his goal. He looked for the perfect final piece. When he found a tiny, sparkling white pebble, he smiled. He placed it on the very top. It stayed! His beautiful wishing tower was finished. He worked hard, and he did not give up. 

His mother walked over and gave him a big, warm hug. "You set a goal and worked until it was done," she said softly. 

The stars began to blink in the night sky. Barnaby looked at his tower, which glowed under the moon. He felt proud and very sleepy. He curled up under the tall trees with his mother. It was time for a deep, peaceful sleep.
