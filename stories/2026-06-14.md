# Pip’s Wise Choice

**Date:** June 14, 2026  
**Theme:** spending wisely vs. impulse buying  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Pip the little squirrel sat on a high tree branch. It was a warm June evening. His grandfather **gave** him three shiny silver seeds. "It is **time** **to** **go** to the forest market," grandfather said. "You can buy a treat."

Pip had a **wish**. **He** wanted a cozy green moss blanket for his bed. It would keep him warm when the night **made** the air cool.

At the market, Pip saw **many** bright things. A blue bird was selling shiny red berries. "**Come** buy **these** berries!" the bird sang. **Their** sweet smell filled the air. Pip reached for his seeds. But then **he** stopped. If **he** bought the berries, **he** would **not** have enough seeds for the blanket. The berries would be gone in one bite.

Next, **he** saw a spinning wooden top. It spun round and round on a leaf. "Is this **your** choice?" asked the friendly chipmunk. Pip wanted **to** say yes. But **he** knew the top would soon sit on a shelf. It was **not** what **he** really wanted. 

"I will wait," Pip whispered **to** himself. 

**He** walked all the way to the end of the path. There, Mr. Owl had the soft green moss blanket. Pip **gave** him the three shiny seeds. 

"You **made** a very wise choice," Mr. Owl said with a smile. "This blanket **has** enough warmth to last a long **time**."

Pip carried his new blanket home. **He** felt so happy. **He** did **not** miss the berries. **He** did **not** want the toy. "**My** bed is so soft now," Pip thought. **He** snuggled under the cozy moss. It was the perfect **time** **to** close his eyes. The forest was quiet, and Pip was ready for sweet dreams.
