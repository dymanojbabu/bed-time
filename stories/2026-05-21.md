# The Glowing Dream Star

**Date:** May 21, 2026  
**Theme:** counting coins and making change  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Oliver tucked his small hands **in** his cozy pockets. He felt the cool, round coins jingle. Tonight, he was visiting Mr. Hoot’s tree shop. Mr. Hoot sold soft, glowing dream stars.

Oliver looked **around** the shop at **all** the beautiful lights. He wanted to buy one special star to help him sleep. He found a small, blue star that beamed softly. Mr. Hoot used a yellow pencil to **write** the price on **a** little tag.

"This blue star is eight cents," Mr. Hoot **said** with a warm smile.

Oliver **had** a shiny ten-cent coin. He knew he **would** need some change back. He did not want to count too **fast**. He wanted to make sure he got it **right**.

"I **have** ten cents," Oliver **said** proudly. He **gave** the silver dime to the wise old owl.

"Perfect," **said** Mr. Hoot. "Ten is **more** than eight. Let us count the change together."

Mr. Hoot placed the dime on the wooden counter. "Eight cents for the star," he counted. He then placed one copper penny down. "Nine." He placed another copper penny down. "Ten! That is the **way** we make change."

Oliver smiled. "Two cents back!"

"Yes," **said** Mr. Hoot. "That is **a** difference of two cents. You are a great counter."

Oliver felt very happy. He knew **which** coins he had now. He put his new blue star and his two quiet pennies **way** down in his pocket. He said goodbye to Mr. Hoot and walked back home under the silver moon.

In his warm bed, Oliver watched his new blue star glow on his table. There was no **more** counting to do tonight. His eyes felt very heavy. He closed them, safe and warm, and drifted off to sleep.
