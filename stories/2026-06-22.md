# Pippin’s Sweet Summer Bite

**Date:** June 22, 2026  
**Theme:** trying new foods  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Pippin the hedgehog loved the warm forest. Today was June twenty-second, the longest day of the year. He and his family sat on a soft blanket **for** **their** evening picnic. The sun was setting, painting the sky in pink and gold. 

Mama Hedgehog set down a wooden bowl of round, purple berries. They looked very different from Pippin’s favorite green clover. 

"What **are** those?" Pippin asked, pulling his spikes in close. 

"These are sun-berries," Mama said. "They grew **all** spring. **Will** you try one?"

Pippin shook his head. He **did** not think he liked purple food. It looked **so** new and strange. He only wanted his usual green leaves. 

Papa Hedgehog smiled. "You **could** just take a **small** bite, Pippin," Papa whispered. "You do not have to eat the whole thing. Just try a tiny **part** of it."

The first fireflies began to glow in the grass. 

"The forest is sharing **its** summer magic with **us**," Mama said softly. "We always try one new thing **before** we go to bed."

Pippin looked at the little berry. He sniffed **its** sweet, fruity scent. It smelled like warm sunshine. He took a deep breath, opened his mouth, and took a tiny nibble. *Pop!* A rush of sweet, cool juice filled his mouth. It was delicious!

"I **did** it!" Pippin whispered, his eyes wide with joy. "I tried it, and I love it!"

"I knew you **could**," Papa said, brushing Pippin's soft forehead. 

Pippin finished the berry. He was **so** glad **that** he tried something new. Now, the **long** day was finally over. The moon was high, and Pippin curled into a cozy ball, dreaming of sweet purple berries as he drifted off to sleep.
