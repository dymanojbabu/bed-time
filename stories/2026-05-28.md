# Leo and the Shiny Blue Pig

**Date:** May 28, 2026  
**Theme:** saving money and piggy banks  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The late May night was warm and quiet. Leo sat by his bedroom window, watching the first stars begin to shine. On his desk sat Penny, his favorite small piggy bank. Penny was made of shiny blue clay and had a happy little smile. 

Leo wanted to buy a big, beautiful book about outer space. To get the book, he needed a few more coins. Earlier that day, Dad gave Leo three shiny silver quarters for helping pull weeds in the garden. Leo also found a lost dime from under his toy box. He picked up all the coins, holding them in his hand. They felt cool and heavy. 

Before he went to bed, Leo wanted to put his money safely away. One by one, he slipped the coins into the slot. *Clink! Clank! Plop!* He loved that happy sound. Leo knew that saving his money was like planting a little seed. If he did not spend these coins on small toys today, his savings would grow. Soon, he would have enough for his special book. 

Leo got out his green notepad. He used a yellow pencil to write down his new total. It was fun to write his numbers and watch them grow bigger. He picked up the blue pig with both hands and gave it a very gentle shake. *Clink-clink!* It was getting heavier. 

Saving his money made him feel proud and happy. He knew he would get to walk to the bookstore with his mom very soon. 

Leo put Penny back on the shelf, right where the moonlight could touch her. He climbed into his soft bed and pulled his cozy blankets up to his chin. He felt so safe and warm. As his eyes closed, he dreamed of flying to the stars, flying high into the quiet, sleepy night.
