# The Cozy Acorn Quilt

**Date:** October 10, 2026  
**Theme:** spending wisely vs. impulse buying  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The autumn moon rose up over Oak Forest, shining like a gold coin. Little Pip the mouse walked down the leaf-covered path with his mother. In his pocket, he held a small pouch of acorn caps. Pip had saved them for a long time. Tonight was the Fall Fair, and he had a goal.

Pip wanted a soft moss quilt to keep him warm all winter.

At the fair, bright lanterns danced in the breeze. Pip heard music that made him laugh. Then, something caught his eye. A table was full of glittering crystal marbles.

"Look at those!" Pip gasped. "May I buy one?"

A kind badger smiled from behind the table. Pip reached into his pouch. He had just the right number of acorn caps to buy one marble. But if he bought it, his pouch would be completely empty.

His mother knelt down beside him. "You may choose, Pip," she said gently. "Will you use that marble tomorrow, or do you know another way to spend your treasure?"

Pip looked closely at the marble. It was pretty, but he would only roll it a few times before forgetting about it. He pictured the chilly winter coming soon. Then he remembered the soft quilt Mrs. Owl made by hand. 

"I want to wait," Pip said softly. He gave the badger a polite smile and kept his acorn caps safe in his pocket.

They walked along the winding way until they found Mrs. Owl’s stall. There sat a blanket woven from sweet clover and fluffy cotton. Pip gave her his pouch of caps. She wrapped the blanket around his shoulders. It felt like a warm hug.

"Thank you for being so wise," his mother whispered. "Now let us head home."

The night wind began to call through the tall trees. In his cozy nest, Pip pulled the soft quilt all the way up to his chin. He knew he had made the best choice. With a calm, happy heart, he closed his eyes, ready for a deep and peaceful sleep.
