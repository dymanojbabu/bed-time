# The Sparkling Path

**Date:** May 08, 2026  
**Theme:** teamwork makes things easier  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Mina the mouse looked at her garden. It was a warm evening in May, and the flowers were blooming. But there was a big job to do before bed. A giant pile of shiny blue pebbles lay in the middle of the green grass. Mina wanted to make them into a pretty path before the moon came out.

"I will start now," she said. Mina tried to lift one pebble. It was very heavy. Then she tried to pull two pebbles, but it took a long time just to move them an inch. The sun was starting to go down, turning the sky pink and orange. She felt a little bit sad. That pile was just too big for one small mouse to move alone.

Just then, Benny the Rabbit hopped over the fence. "May I come and help you?" Benny asked with a kind smile. "I have my little red wagon. I can put the stones in there for you."

Mina felt so happy! "Yes, please!" she said. 

Benny pulled his wagon near the pile. First, they had to wash the garden dirt off the stones. Mina dipped them in a bucket of cool water, and then Benny dried them with a big, soft leaf. That made the pebbles sparkle like little stars. 

Mina would lift a stone, and Benny would carry it to the path. Working together was very easy. The big pile began to melt away quickly. Every stone has a special place on the path now. Because they were a team, the work did not feel like a chore. It felt like a fun game.

"Look!" Mina cried. The path was finished. It was time to see how it glowed in the dark. The blue pebbles looked like a river of light leading to her front door.

"Thank you, Benny," Mina said. "Teamwork made this fast and fun."

Benny nodded and waved. It was time for bed. Mina went into her cozy house, and Benny hopped home to his burrow. The garden was quiet and still. Mina closed her eyes, feeling peaceful. She knew that with a friend, any big job can become a small one. The world was soft, the path was bright, and it was time to dream.
