# Benny’s Sparkly Smile Dance

**Date:** June 01, 2026  
**Theme:** brushing teeth and healthy habits  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Once, in a cozy mossy home, lived Benny the little bear. Benny was a bear who loved to play all day in the sunny woods, but he also loved his quiet bedtime routine. He liked to know that his teeth were clean and strong before he went to sleep. 

Every night, Benny walked to the sparkling sink with his favorite green toothbrush. 

"It is time for the tooth-brushing dance!" Benny whispered happily. 

He made a little song to help him brush for two whole minutes, moving the brush gently and not too fast. 

"Up, down, round and round, we brush each tooth with care!" Benny sang. 

Benny put a small, minty dot of toothpaste onto his brush. He did not need to use too much, just a tiny bit. He began to brush. Swish, swish, swish! So many tiny, fluffy bubbles filled his mouth. They looked like happy little clouds. Benny made sure to brush the front, the back, and the flat tops of every single tooth. 

"We must wash away all the day's treats," his mother said with a warm smile. 

After brushing, Benny took a sip of cool, clean water to rinse. He spit the water into the sink. Splash! He looked in the mirror and smiled. His teeth were as clean as the bright stars outside. 

"I wish my teeth could glow in the dark!" Benny laughed, climbing into his warm, soft bed. 

His mother tucked his blanket in close. "They will shine bright tomorrow," she said, kissing his forehead. "Because you take such good care of them."

Benny felt cozy, warm, and very clean. He closed his eyes, took a deep breath, and drifted off into sweet, happy dreams.
