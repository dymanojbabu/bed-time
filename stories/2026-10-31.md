# Nora’s Glowing Goal

**Date:** October 31, 2026  
**Theme:** setting a goal and working toward it  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

On the last day of October, Nora had a special plan. She had been thinking about it all week. 

"Today, I have a goal," Nora told her father. "I will make five paper lanterns to light our garden path for Halloween night."

"That is a lovely goal," he said with a warm smile. "Take your time, and do one step at a time."

Nora knew what she needed to do. First, she folded thick orange paper. Then, she drew friendly, smiling faces on each sheet. It took patience. Sometimes her fingers felt tired, but she did not give up. She focused on one lantern at a time. She smiled as she finished lantern number one, then two, then three and four. 

When the sun began to dip, her brother came outside to see her work. He gave Nora a cozy cup of warm apple cider. 

"Which one is your favorite?" he asked.

Nora pointed to the very last lantern, decorated with a sleepy crescent moon. "This is my favorite," she whispered happily. "I set a goal, and I worked until it was done."

As twilight settled in on this cool October 31, Nora placed each little lantern along the quiet stone path. Inside each one, a small battery candle flickered safely. Each lantern shared its warm, golden light with the dry leaves on the ground. 

Soon, friendly neighbors walked down the street in their cozy autumn coats. They smiled when they saw the glowing path. 

"What a gentle sight," someone said softly.

Nora sat on the front porch, wrapped up in a fuzzy blanket. Above her, a number of silver stars twinkled in the clear sky, which stretched out like a dark velvet blanket. She could see each little light glowing softly in the dusk. Nora gave a long, quiet yawn. She had worked hard, and her goal was complete. Now, everything was peaceful and still. 

Close your eyes, feel the cozy autumn breeze, and drift into sweet, quiet dreams.
