# Mia’s Warm June Truth

**Date:** June 16, 2026  
**Theme:** being honest even when it is hard  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

It was a **very** warm June night. Outside, the summer crickets began to **sing** their sweet bedtime song. Mia sat on **her** cozy bed, but she could not **go** to sleep. Her heart was beating **fast**. 

Earlier that day, Mia and her little puppy **were** playing near the porch. Mia wanted to **open** her mother’s garden box to see the seeds. But **when** she **did**, she bumped **one** pretty blue flower pot. It fell **and** broke on the ground. Mia was so scared! She hid the broken pieces behind a bush. 

Now, looking out her window, Mia made a **wish**. "I **wish** I could fix the pot," she whispered to the stars. 

Mia knew she had to tell the truth, even **if** it was hard. Sometimes it takes **work** to be brave. She got out of bed and walked to her mother’s room. Her mother was reading a book. 

"Mom," Mia said, her voice very quiet. "I broke your blue flower pot today. I was scared, so I hid it. I am so sorry."

Her mother smiled and pulled Mia into a big, soft hug. "Thank you for being honest, Mia," her mother said. "It is hard to speak the truth when we are afraid. But your honesty is a beautiful gift."

Mia felt a warm, happy sigh escape her chest. The heavy feeling in her heart was totally gone. Her mother walked Mia back to bed **and** tucked her under the soft sheets. 

"The bright June moon will watch **us** tonight," her mother whispered, kissing Mia’s forehead. "Sleep well, my brave girl."

Mia closed her eyes. Her mind was quiet and her heart was light. She was ready to drift off to sleep, listening to the crickets sing in the gentle night.
