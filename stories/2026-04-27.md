# Leo’s Tall Stone Tower

**Date:** April 27, 2026  
**Theme:** finishing what you start  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Leo the turtle sat by the blue river on a quiet spring evening. It was April 27, 2026, and the air was very warm and smelled like sweet flowers. "Look at all these stones," Leo said to his friend, Pip the frog. "I can build the tallest tower in the whole woods!"

First, Leo found a very big, flat rock for the bottom. Then he found many round, grey stones to stack on top. He worked very hard, moving slowly. But after a while, Leo got tired. A bright yellow butterfly flew past his nose. It looked like so much fun to chase.

"I do not want to build anymore," Leo sighed. "It takes a long time and my shell feels heavy." He looked at the butterfly. "We should go chase it, Pip! We can leave the stones here."

Just then, Leo’s mom walked by. She stopped and smiled at the little tower. "Come here, Leo," she said softly. "How will you feel tomorrow if you leave your tower half-done? You have worked so hard on his base."

Leo looked at his pile of stones. He was only three rocks away from the top. He knew she was right. He could do it if he just stayed a little longer. He did not want to leave his work unfinished.

Leo sat back down. He did not laugh or play with the butterfly yet. Instead, he carefully picked up the last few stones. He placed them one by one, very slowly. The tower stood steady and tall.

"I did it!" Leo cheered. "I finished what I started!"

Pip clapped his little green hands. The sun began to set, painting the sky in pink and purple. Leo felt a happy glow inside because he did not quit. Finishing his project felt even better than chasing the butterfly.

Now that his work was done, Leo was ready to rest. He crawled into his cozy bed of soft green moss. The river made a sleepy sound like a song. Leo closed his eyes, feeling calm and proud. He was ready for a peaceful sleep.
