# Pip’s Cozy Summer Share

**Date:** July 15, 2026  
**Theme:** sharing with friends  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

On a warm July night, Pip the chipmunk found a very special giant leaf. It was as soft as a cloud. He wanted to sit under it all by himself. 

"This is my cozy leaf," Pip said. He wrapped it around his little shoulders. It felt like a warm hug. He wanted to tell everyone how nice it felt to be so warm.

Just then, his best friend Penny the mouse walked by. Her whiskers were shaking in the cool breeze. The summer night was getting a little chilly. Pip looked at her. He thought about his warm leaf. At first, he wanted to hide it behind a tree. He wanted to keep all the cozy warmth for himself. But Pip loved Penny. He knew sharing would make her happy.

"Do you want to share?" Pip asked.

Penny smiled. Pip gave a big piece of the soft leaf to her. It was big enough for both of them! Now, Penny had a warm blanket, too. It made him feel so good inside to see her happy.

"Thank you," Penny whispered. "We can snuggle together and look at the stars."

They lay down side by side on the soft green moss. The yellow moon goes up high into the sky, shining down with a gentle, sleepy light. The crickets began to sing a sweet lullaby. Pip knew that sharing made the night feel even warmer. He closed his eyes, ready to sleep, drifting off into happy dreams with his friend by his side.
