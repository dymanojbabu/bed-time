# Oliver's Shiny Coins

**Date:** November 13, 2026  
**Theme:** earning money by doing chores  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Outside, crisp autumn leaves danced all around the yard. Oliver looked at his little glass jar sitting on the table. 

"Mom, may I earn some coins for my jar today?" he asked.

Mom smiled a very warm smile. "You can help me with our chores," she said. "Working hard is the best way to earn them."

First, they worked in the kitchen together. Mom washed the soup bowls, and Oliver dried them. "You use your towel so carefully," Mom told him with a soft laugh. Oliver set each clean bowl into its spot. Clink! Mom dropped one bright, shiny coin into his hand. "Thank you for being such a wonderful helper," she said.

Next, Oliver stepped outside to sweep the front porch. The birds sang their quiet evening songs from the big oak tree. Oliver hummed along as the broom went swoosh, swoosh across the porch. Then, he neatly lined up the boots by the door. His sister came out to call him inside as the chilly breeze began to blow.

"Look at this tidy porch," Dad said, smiling. Clink! Another coin joined the first one in the jar. 

Once the chores were all finished, Oliver held his jar up to the soft lamp light. He had worked hard today, and earning his own coins made him feel happy and proud. Helping his family was a wonderful feeling.

Now, the November stars began to blink in the dark, peaceful sky. Oliver slipped into his cozy pajamas and climbed into bed. He listened to the wind rustling the branches outside. He pulled his blanket up to his chin, feeling very warm and relaxed. The house was quiet, still, and safe. With a peaceful heart, Oliver closed his eyes and drifted off into a deep, gentle sleep.
