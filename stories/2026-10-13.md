# The Autumn Leaf Bed

**Date:** October 13, 2026  
**Theme:** teamwork makes things easier  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The October air was cool and sweet as the golden sun dipped behind the hills. Little Bear Bramble wanted to build a soft bed of autumn leaves inside his cozy den. He looked out at the forest floor. Crisp red and yellow leaves were everywhere, dancing in the gentle breeze.

Bramble picked up three leaves in his small paws. He tried to carry them inside, but a gust of wind blew them away. He tried again, working hard to scoop up more. It was slow work, and his arms were growing tired. Soon, the stars would begin to shine.

"Do you want some help?" called Pip the rabbit. Pip hopped out from behind an old oak tree.

"I do not know if I can finish before bedtime," Bramble sighed. "The forest has so many leaves, but I am only one little bear."

"We can do it together," Pip said with a warm smile. "Teamwork will make the job easy for us."

Pip had a clever plan. "Let us use this wide piece of bark like a sled. We can each hold one side."

Bramble smiled. Pip held his side, and Bramble held his. Together, they walked across the soft moss. Bramble would put a handful of red leaves on the bark, and Pip would add some golden ones. With two friends working as a team, their little sled was piled high in no time at all.

They pulled it to the den and tipped the leaves inside. Each trip was faster and more fun than the last. Soon, Bramble had a deep, cozy bed that smelled like sweet pine and autumn rain.

"Thank you, Pip," Bramble whispered, letting out a big yawn. "You made everything so much easier."

"Good friends always share their help," Pip whispered back.

Pip hopped home to his burrow nearby. Bramble climbed into his leaf bed. He loved to sit quietly and watch the silver moon rise above the treetops. Feeling safe, warm, and happy, Bramble closed his eyes and drifted off to sleep.
