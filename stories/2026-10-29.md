# Mia’s Helpful Hands

**Date:** October 29, 2026  
**Theme:** earning money by doing chores  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The October wind blew softly outside the window, painting the trees in red and gold. Inside the warm house, Mia shook her little glass jar. It was empty, but she had a plan. She wanted to earn shiny coins of her own. 

"Mom, can I do some chores today?" Mia asked. 

Mom gave her a gentle smile. "Yes, Mia. You can help me get the house ready for evening. You may use the little red rake outside, and then we will look for more to do."

Mia zipped her coat and stepped out into the crisp yard. The grass had more fallen leaves than yesterday. There was no breeze right now, so each leaf rested quietly on the lawn. Mia began to sweep them together. Soon, all the orange and yellow leaves formed one big, tidy pile. 

Next, Mia went back into the kitchen. She put each pair of muddy boots by the door in a neat row. Then, she picked up a soft cloth to wipe the dinner table until its smooth surface shone in the lamplight. 

Mia felt her arms grow heavy, but her heart felt light and happy. Helping her family was hard work, but it was fun too. 

Mom walked in and clapped her hands softly. "Look at this room! I can tell you were very careful, Mia."

Mom reached into her pocket and placed three shiny coins into Mia’s open hand. 

Mia smiled a big, sleepy smile. She dropped each coin into her jar. Clink, clink, clink went the metal. The sound was soft and sweet. She wondered what she would save her coins for, but right now, her eyes felt very heavy. 

Mia climbed into bed and pulled her warm quilt up to her chin. The moon peeked through the glass, shining its silver glow over her neat room. With no more chores left to do, Mia closed her eyes and drifted into a cozy, peaceful sleep.
