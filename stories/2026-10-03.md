# Bramble’s Golden Nest

**Date:** October 03, 2026  
**Theme:** setting a goal and working toward it  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Bramble the little hedgehog had a big goal for autumn. He wanted to make the softest, most peaceful nest in the whole forest. It felt like a very big project for a small hedgehog, but Bramble knew that every big dream starts with a plan.

With a little twig, Bramble began to write his plan in the soft dirt under an oak tree. Step one: find dry oak leaves. Step two: find green moss. Step three: put it all together so he could sleep through the chilly nights. 

"It takes time and work to build something special," his mother whispered with a smile. "Just do one small part each day."

On the first day, Bramble went looking for leaves. He saw bright red leaves on a rock, but these were too wet from the morning rain. Then he found a quiet pile of golden maple leaves tucked beneath a bush. They were dry and crisp. The gentle crunching sound under his tiny paws made him laugh. He carried a neat bundle back to his hollow log. 

On the second day, Bramble searched for green moss. He found soft moss growing beside the bubbling brook. He gathered up those fluffy green patches and carried them home. Now his little nest was really beginning to take shape. 

On the third day, Bramble carefully tucked each leaf and moss cushion into place. He lined the inside so it would feel warm and snug. "This is our cozy corner of the woods," Bramble thought happily as the evening shadows grew long.

He was tired from his busy days, but he had reached his goal. Little by little, day by day, his hard effort had paid off. 

Bramble curled his small paws and decided to sit right in the center of his golden nest. It was soft like a cloud. The autumn stars began to blink above the trees, shining down like gentle nightlights. Bramble closed his sleepy eyes, took a deep breath, and drifted off into sweet dreams.
