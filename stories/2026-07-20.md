# Bella’s Thank You Star

**Date:** July 20, 2026  
**Theme:** gratitude and saying thank you  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Bella was a small rabbit with very soft ears. She loved to run in the green grass all day. But now, the yellow sun was going down. It was time to go to sleep.

Bella sat on her cozy bed. She looked out the window. The birds were flying back to their trees to rest. Bella could see the first bright star blinking in the dark blue sky. 

"I know what I want to do before I close my eyes," Bella whispered to her mother. 

"What is that, my sweet bunny?" her mother asked with a warm smile.

"I want to say thank you," Bella said. "I have so many nice things that make me happy."

Her mother hugged her close. "That is a lovely way to end the day. It fills our hearts with peace."

Bella looked around her cozy room. "Thank you, blue blanket," she said. "Thank you for keeping me warm." She touched the soft sheets with her small paws. "Thank you, fluffy pillow, for being so soft."

Then, Bella looked out the window. "Thank you, stars, for shining so bright. And thank you, yellow moon, for your gentle light."

She thought about the sweet orange carrots she ate for dinner and the nice ladybugs she played with today. She wanted to thank them too! It made her chest feel warm and happy. Saying thank you made all the good things feel even better. 

"Do you know what I am thankful for?" her mother asked softly, kissing her forehead. 

"What?" Bella whispered, her eyes getting very heavy.

"I am thankful for you," her mother said. "You make my world so happy."

Bella felt so loved. She did not have any more words left, but she had a happy smile. Now, she was ready to go to sleep. She closed her eyes, feeling warm and safe, and drifted off to sleep under the quiet stars.
