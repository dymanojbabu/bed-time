# Pip’s Big Climb

**Date:** May 07, 2026  
**Theme:** setting a goal and working toward it  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Pip the Squirrel lived in a big, leafy oak tree. One warm evening, he looked up at the very top branch. It was the highest branch in the whole forest.

"I want to climb all the way up there," Pip said to his mom. "I want to see the moon from the highest leaf. It looks so bright and pretty."

"That is a great goal," Mom said. "It will take work, but I know you can do it. We can practice every day."

Pip started to climb right away. He moved fast at first, but then his little legs got tired. He looked down and felt a little worried because the ground was far away. He crawled out to a middle branch and sighed.

"I am not at the top yet," Pip said.

"It is okay," Mom told him. "We do not have to get there today. We can try again tomorrow. Just keep your eyes on the goal."

The next day, Pip was ready. He thought about how to use his tiny claws to grip the bark. He took one step, and then another. He did not try to go too fast this time. He took his time and felt the rough wood under his paws.

Pip felt strong. He began to laugh with joy as he hopped over a big knot in the tree. He was doing it! Each day, he climbed a little bit higher than the day before. He knew that if he kept trying, he would reach the top.

Finally, on the third night, Pip reached the highest branch. He could see the whole forest. The air was cool, but his thick fur felt warm. The stars looked like little diamonds.

"Look at us!" Pip shouted to his mom. They were both at the top now.

"You worked hard every day," Mom said. "You had a goal and you worked until you reached it."

The sun went down, and the moon rose high in the sky. Pip felt very sleepy and very proud. He curled his tail around his body. It was time for us to sleep in our high, happy home.
