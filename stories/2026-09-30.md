# Penny and the Starlight Book

**Date:** September 30, 2026  
**Theme:** spending wisely vs. impulse buying  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Penny walked through the evening market with one silver coin tucked in her warm pocket. It was the last day of September, and golden leaves danced all around her boots. She had saved her special coin all month.

Near the front of the market, a table was covered in bright spinning tops. They flashed neon colors, and Penny could hear their loud buzzing hum. Penny reached into her pocket. It felt so exciting to start spending right away. She almost bought the glittery toy on impulse.

But when she stopped and watched, one of the tops tumbled off the stand and stopped spinning. Penny looked down at her silver coin. The flashing toy looked fun right now, but would she still care about it tomorrow? 

Penny decided to wait. She took a slow breath and walked a little further along the path. Then, near a soft lantern, she found a cozy wooden cart filled with painted storybooks and warm wool blankets. 

“Welcome,” said the shopkeeper with a gentle smile. “These are made to bring us joy on cool autumn nights.”

Penny saw a midnight-blue book with silver stars on the cover. “May I look inside?” she asked.

“You may,” the keeper replied.

Penny turned the smooth pages. There were peaceful bedtime tales that she could read together with her family all through the winter. A calm, happy feeling settled in her chest. Penny knew this was the right choice. She gave her one silver coin to the keeper and hugged the book close to her heart.

Back home, Penny climbed into her soft bed. The noisy market toys were already forgotten, but her wonderful book rested right beside her pillow. She closed her eyes as the quiet night wind whispered around the window. Knowing she had chosen wisely made her feel safe, cozy, and proud. With one gentle sigh, she drifted happily off to sleep.
