# The Great Tire Swing

**Date:** May 05, 2026  
**Theme:** taking turns and being patient  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The sun was starting to set over the soft, green hills on this quiet Tuesday evening. Pip the Bunny and Squeak the Squirrel went to **find** the big tire swing hanging **by** the old oak tree. It was the best swing in the whole forest.

"I want to go first!" **said** Pip, his long ears twitching with excitement.

"No, I want to go first!" Squeak **said**, his bushy tail shaking. 

They both looked at the swing. **In** the quiet woods, they had to decide **which** friend **would** jump on first. Both friends felt a little grumpy because they did not want to wait. It is hard to be patient when you want to play.

Pip took a deep breath and remembered what his mama told him. "Let **us** take turns," he **said** softly. "I will swing for ten big pushes, and then you can **try**. It is much better than fighting."

Squeak nodded his head. He knew **that** was a fair idea. Pip hopped **in** the tire. Squeak began to count out loud. **How** high could Pip go? 

"One, two, three..." Squeak called out. 

Pip felt **like** a colorful bird flying through the cool air. He could **see** the bluebells closing their petals for the night. He could **see** the tiny stars starting to twinkle. 

When Squeak got to ten, Pip hopped out right away. He did not ask for more. Now it was Squeak’s turn. Pip gave him a big, gentle push. **We** are a great team, Pip thought. He watched his friend smile as he zoomed back and forth. 

**These** moments were very special. Taking turns meant nobody felt left out. There was **no** reason to be sad when you have a friend to wait with. 

By the time the silver moon was high, both friends were very sleepy. Their eyes were getting heavy. 

"I **like** our swing," Squeak whispered as they walked home. "And I like **how** we shared today."

"Me too," Pip **said**.

They reached their cozy burrows. The forest was calm and dark. It was time for **us** all to sleep and dream of tomorrow's adventures. Goodnight, little friends.
