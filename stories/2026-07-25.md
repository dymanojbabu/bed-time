# The Warmest Blanket

**Date:** July 25, 2026  
**Theme:** giving to others who need help  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Once upon a time, in a small, green forest, lived a little squirrel named Pip. Pip loved to work hard every summer day. He would start his morning early, scurrying around to get soft moss and silky flower petals from the meadow. Pip made a giant, cozy pile of treasures inside his favorite oak tree.

"I have so many nice things," Pip said to himself with a happy grin. He looked around his warm nest. He had gathered more than any other animal in the wood. 

As the summer sun began to drop low, Pip saw his neighbor, Barnaby the badger. Barnaby was walking very slowly. He could not move fast like the other forest people. Barnaby had spent all afternoon trying to build a nest, but he was tired, and he did not have any soft moss to lie on. 

Pip looked at his own big pile of soft moss, and then he looked at his tired friend. Pip knew he can help those who need a little extra love. 

Pip gathered a large armful of his softest moss and three big pink flower petals. He scurried over to Barnaby. "Here," Pip said gently. "I made this soft bed for you."

Barnaby smiled a big, sleepy smile. "Thank you, Pip. Now I can rest so comfortably tonight."

Giving his moss to a friend in need made Pip feel warmer inside than a sweet cup of berry tea. Giving from the heart was the best feeling in the whole world.

So, as the night sky turned dark and full of twinkling stars, Pip climbed back into his own hollow tree. The woodland crickets began to sing their soft bedtime song. Pip snuggled down, happy and cozy, knowing his friend was warm too. 

He closed his eyes, took a deep breath, and drifted fast asleep.
