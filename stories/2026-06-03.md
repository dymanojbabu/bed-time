# Oliver’s Thank-You Walk

**Date:** June 03, 2026  
**Theme:** gratitude and saying thank you  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The sun was going down on a warm June evening. Oliver the little bear cub looked out of his cozy cave. The forest was peaceful and quiet. Before it was time to sleep, he wanted to take a short, quiet walk.

As he walked under the green branches, Oliver thought about his happy day. He looked up at the tall oak trees. "Thank you for your cool shade today," he whispered to them. A gentle breeze blew from the soft hills. Does the wind hear his thank you? It seemed to blow a warm hello right back. Oliver smiled. He knew that if you say thank you, your heart feels very happy and warm. 

Next, Oliver walked past the blue river. He sat down and watched the water splash over the shiny stones. "Thank you for the cool water to splash in," he said to the river.

Soon, Oliver saw his mother. She gave him a sweet, ripe strawberry to eat before bed. It was the best treat of the day. "Thank you, Mama, for the sweet snack," Oliver said. 

Mama bear brushed his soft ears and hugged him tight. "You are very welcome, little one," she said with a big smile.

Now, all the other forest animals were getting ready for bed, too. The little birds stopped their singing. They tucked their heads under their wings. The sleepy flowers closed their petals. 

Oliver walked back inside his cave and climbed into his bed of soft green moss. He kept his eyes open just long enough to look out at the dark sky. A bright silver star was shining. 

"Thank you for the beautiful day," Oliver whispered to the night. 

He pulled his cozy leaf blanket up to his chin. His eyes felt very heavy now. With a happy and grateful heart, he drifted off to sleep, ready for sweet dreams.
