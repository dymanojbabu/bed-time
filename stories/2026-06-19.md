# The Great Leaf Blanket

**Date:** June 19, 2026  
**Theme:** teamwork makes things easier  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The warm June sun was going down behind the tall pine trees. It was almost time for sleep, but Pip and his brother, Pop, were not ready. The evening air was turning a little cold, and they wanted to build a cozy nest of soft leaves in their favorite tree.

Pip tried to carry a giant oak leaf all by himself. It was much bigger than he was. He tried to pull it, but he fell over. He shook the dust off his whiskers. "This is too hard," Pip sighed. "How will we ever get our bed ready before the stars come out?"

Pop ran over with a big smile. "I know what to do," Pop said. "If we work together, we can do it!"

Pop grabbed one side of the giant leaf, and Pip grabbed the other side. They lifted it together. It was so much lighter now! They carried it easily to the tree. Next, they needed soft moss. Each mouse took a turn searching the forest floor. Pip found a patch of green moss, and Pop carried it up. Then, Pop found some fluffy feathers, and Pip brought them inside. 

By working as a team, their nest was finished in no time. It was the softest bed they had ever made. They did not need to read a book to learn that teamwork makes everything easier; they could feel it in their warm, cozy home. 

The two little mice curled up together under their giant leaf blanket. They listened to the gentle wind in the trees. They were warm, safe, and happy. As their eyes closed, they knew they would always be the best team. Goodnight, little mice, sleep tight.
