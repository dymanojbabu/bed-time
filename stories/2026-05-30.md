# Pip’s Cozy Goal

**Date:** May 30, 2026  
**Theme:** setting a goal and working toward it  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Once upon a time, a little brown squirrel named Pip had a big dream. He wanted to build his **own** cozy hammock high in the tall willow tree. He did not need to **buy** anything from a store. He just needed to work hard. "I **have** a goal," Pip whispered to the golden sunshine. "**First**, I must gather soft green moss and strong, bendy twigs." Pip knew this **will** take a **number** of days, but he was ready.

Every morning, Pip woke **up** with a happy plan. He did not **sit** still. He searched the forest floor for the best materials. He found **all** the softest leaves. His mother helped **him** find long, safe vines. Other forest **people**, like the rabbit and the deer, walked by and smiled. They asked **him** **what** he was making. "I **call** it my dream bed," Pip said proudly. He did not rush. Every day, he added a little more. He knew that working step-by-step is how we reach our goals. It felt good to watch his hammock grow.

**Now**, the hammock was finally finished. It hung safely between two branches. It was soft, round, and perfectly cozy. Pip could **sit** in it and swing gently in the breeze. He felt so happy inside because he had worked hard for his dream. He did not give **up**, even when his paws were tired. He looked **about** at the quiet, green forest, feeling proud of his hard work.

The sun began to set, painting the sky in soft pink and sleepy purple. Pip climbed into his new, warm hammock. It hugged **him** just right. As the first silver stars came out, Pip closed his tired eyes. He had worked hard today, and **now** it was time to rest. Sleep sweet, Pip. Goodnight, stars.
