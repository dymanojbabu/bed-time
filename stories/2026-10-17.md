# The Brave Little Shadow

**Date:** October 17, 2026  
**Theme:** being brave when scared  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

October leaves danced outside the bedroom window. The autumn wind sang a low, whistling song through the trees. Little Mia pulled her warm blanket up to her chin. In the corner of our cozy room, a tall, dark shadow swayed against the wall.

Mia felt her heart thump fast. She did not like the dark when the night wind blew so loudly.

"Look at that," Mia whispered to her big brother, Leo. "What if there is a spooky giant in our corner?"

Leo smiled gently and sat beside her on the bed. "You have a very brave heart, Mia," he said in a quiet voice. "Being brave does not mean we never feel afraid. It just means we take a deep breath, even when things look strange to us."

Mia took a slow, deep breath. The air felt cool, then warm inside her chest. Could she really be brave? 

"Let us go find out what is making that shape together," Leo said, holding out his hand. 

Mia held on tight and stepped onto the rug. Step by step, they walked across the floor. When they reached the window, Mia peeked past Leo's shoulder. 

She looked at the wall, and then she looked at the windowsill. There was no giant at all! It was only Mia’s potted plant with its many round leaves, dancing in the light of the streetlamp. 

Mia giggled, and the nervous feeling floated right out of her chest. "It was just my plant!"

"You see?" Leo said softly. "The dark can play tricks on us, but you always have the courage to look closer."

Mia climbed back under her covers. The room felt peaceful and quiet once again. High above, many silver stars began to glow in the night sky. She knew that if she ever felt scared again, she could always find her brave breath. 

"Time to sleep, little one," Leo whispered, tucking her in.

Mia closed her eyes. Wrapped in her warm bed, she smiled and drifted off to sleep.
