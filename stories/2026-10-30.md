# The Shiny Pumpkin

**Date:** October 30, 2026  
**Theme:** taking turns and being patient  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The autumn wind blew softly through the tall oak trees. Outside, the night was crisp and cold, but inside the cottage, a small fire crackled in the hearth. 

Little bear Bramble and his sister Fern sat on the rug. Tomorrow was Halloween, and on the table rested a very round orange pumpkin. 

"Time to clean our pumpkin before bed," Papa Bear said with a warm smile. He handed Fern a soft, wet cloth. "First, we wash the garden dust away."

Fern began to wipe the bumpy rind. Bramble leaned in close, his paws wiggling. "I want to do it right now!" he said. "I want my own turn!"

Papa patted Bramble’s shoulder gently. "Being patient is like waiting for a star to appear," Papa whispered. "It takes a little time, but when we take turns, everyone shares the joy. Watch how careful Fern is."

Bramble took a slow, deep breath. While he waited, he looked out the cozy window. High in the dark sky, something caught his eye. "Look, Fern! Can you find that bright star twinkling over the hill?"

Fern looked up and smiled. "I see it! It looks like a tiny nightlight." 

Fern handed the cloth to her brother. "Thank you for waiting, Bramble. Now you get to make the other side shine."

Bramble wiped the pumpkin until it gleamed like gold in the firelight. He felt proud. Taking turns made the work feel light, and waiting had not been so hard after all.

Papa poured sweet warm cider that Mama had made. It had been a long, happy day, and the little bears began to yawn. 

"You worked so well together," Papa said, tucking them into bed with a thick wool blanket. 

Bramble snuggled down, feeling warm from his nose to his toes. The pumpkin sat quietly by the door, shining softly as both little bears drifted off to sleep.
