# Leo Finishes the Dragon

**Date:** May 04, 2026  
**Theme:** finishing what you start  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Leo sat in his warm corner of the den. He had a big plan. He wanted to write a story about a magic dragon. First, he picked up a green crayon. He began to draw a long tail with many scales. Each scale was bright and shiny.

"Look at his tail!" Leo said to his mom.

"It is very lovely," her voice was soft and kind.

Leo started to draw the wings. But drawing wings was hard. They kept looking like big circles instead of feathers. Leo felt tired. He looked out the window at the tall trees. He thought he may go play outside instead. He wondered how his friends were playing in the green grass. 

"I think I am done," Leo said. "No more drawing for me today."

Mom knelt down beside him. "If you stop now, your dragon will never fly. It can be hard to finish what we start, but do you know how good it feels to see the end?"

Leo looked at the half-finished dragon. He wanted to see it fly through the clouds. He did not want to leave it without wings. Then, he picked up his blue crayon. He did not run off to play. He took his time and worked slowly. He added blue wings. He added a friendly face with a big smile. He even drew a bright sun in the corner of the page.

Each part of the picture was special because he did not quit. When the paper was full, Leo let out a happy sigh. How proud he felt to see the whole dragon! It was the best thing he had ever made. 

"I did it," he said. "I finished my work."

"Yes, you did," Mom said. She gave him a warm hug. 

The sun goes down behind the hills now. Leo put his crayons away in their box. He felt ready to rest because his job was done. He crawled into his bed and closed his eyes. The finished dragon flew through his dreams, and Leo slept very well.
