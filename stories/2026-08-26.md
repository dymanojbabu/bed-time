# The Little Busy Bee

**Date:** August 26, 2026  
**Theme:** helping at home  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The golden sun was beginning to sink low in the warm August sky. It had been a long, cheerful day, but now it was time to get the cozy house ready for rest.

Little Mia looked around her bedroom. Blocks and crayons were scattered across the soft rug. Mia decided it was a wonderful moment to try her best and help out. She wanted to show her family that she could care for her own cozy space.

"I may need a clever game," Mia whispered with a bright smile.

Mia picked a secret number in her head: three! First, she gathered all her wooden blocks into their box. Second, she tucked her stuffed animals gently into their basket. Third, she stacked her picture books in a neat, smooth row. The room was tidy before long because her hands moved like busy little bees.

Next, Mia walked into the kitchen. Her father was clearing the dinner table. 

"May I use the soft towel to dry the spoons?" Mia asked.

"You may," her father replied with a warm smile. "Thank you for helping our home feel so calm."

Mia loved working together. She felt happy inside because helping made the whole evening feel light and sweet. It was fun to lend a hand and care for their home.

Soon, the silver moon rose high in the quiet sky. The chores were all done, and the house was peaceful and clean. It was time to go to sleep.

Mia climbed into her soft bed and pulled up the blanket. Her room was neat, her heart was happy, and a gentle breeze blew outside her window. She closed her eyes, ready to drift into dreamland after a lovely day of doing her very best.
