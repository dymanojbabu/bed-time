# The Summer Meadow Team

**Date:** July 05, 2026  
**Theme:** teamwork makes things easier  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The sun was going down over the sleepy meadow on a warm July evening. Lily the little mouse wanted to make her bed cozy. She had been busy all day, but she still needed soft yellow flowers for her pillow. She walked out into the warm grass to look for them. Soon, she saw her friend, Barnaby the badger. Barnaby has a big, soft nest under the oak tree, but he wanted some sweet berries to eat before sleep. 

"I can see the best yellow flowers, but they are too high up," Lily said. She stretched her tiny paws, but she could not reach them. 

"And I see the sweet berries," Barnaby said, "but they are too deep inside the thorny bush." 

It was hard to do this work alone. Each animal had their own job to do, which felt too big for just one helper. They needed more help. 

"We can help each other!" Lily squeaked. "That is always the best way."

Lily climbed up on Barnaby’s soft back. From there, she could reach up and pick the yellow flowers. She tossed them down into a neat little pile. Next, Lily scrambled deep into the thorny bush. She was so small, she could easily pop the sweet berries off the branches without getting scratched. She rolled them out to her big friend. 

Working together had made the chores so easy! The big jobs felt so small when they shared them. Now, Barnaby had his sweet night snack, and Lily had her soft pillow. They sat under the bright stars, feeling happy, safe, and tired. When you work as a team, you always have a friend to help you. Lily closed her eyes. Barnaby gave a big yawn. It was time to drift off to sleep. Goodnight, little helpers.
