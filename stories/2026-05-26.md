# Barnaby’s Brave Night

**Date:** May 26, 2026  
**Theme:** being brave when scared  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The warm breeze of a sweet May evening blew from the open window. It was the end of a long, happy day, and little Barnaby Bear was ready for bed. Or, at least, he thought he was. 

He tucked his soft green blanket up to his chin. "Mama," Barnaby whispered. "I see funny shapes on the wall. Those shadows look very big."

Mama Bear sat gently on the edge of the bed. "Shadows can look scary," she said. "But they are just shapes from the daytime. Let us look at them together."

Barnaby held Mama's warm paw tightly. He wanted to be brave, but how could he start? He felt a tiny flutter in his tummy. 

"Being brave does not mean you are never scared," Mama said. "It just means taking a deep breath and trying anyway."

"Would you help me?" Barnaby asked.

"I would love to," Mama smiled. "Let us read the shapes on the wall like a bedtime book."

Mama pointed to a big, round shadow. "What do you think that one is about?" she asked.

Barnaby looked closely. He realized it was not a giant monster. It was just his toy frog sitting on the shelf! "It is much smaller than I thought," Barnaby giggled. He felt a little braver already. 

They found some more shadows on the wall. One was just a tree branch waving hello, and another was a sleepy curtain. They were not scary at all. They were just cozy friends dancing in the moonlight. 

"This is my favorite way to look at the dark," Barnaby yawned. He was so proud of himself. He had faced his fears. 

"You were very brave tonight," Mama whispered, kissing his forehead.

Barnaby smiled and closed his eyes. He felt safe, warm, and ready to dream.
