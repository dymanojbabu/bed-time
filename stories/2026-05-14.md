# Leo’s Big Helping Day

**Date:** May 14, 2026  
**Theme:** helping at home  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Leo woke up on May 14 with a big, bright idea. He looked at the sun shining through the window. He thought about all the things his family did to keep their house cozy. Today, he wanted to be a helper!

What should he do first? He looked down at his bedroom floor. His fuzzy socks were in a messy pile. Leo picked them up and put them into the tall laundry basket. It was a small job, but it made his room look very neat. There was no more clutter on the rug. Leo felt proud.

Next, Leo went into the kitchen. He saw his dad washing the morning dishes. Leo grabbed a soft, yellow towel. His dad smiled and handed him a plastic cup. Leo wiped the water off the cup until it was dry and shiny. His dad told him, "Your help is the best surprise I have had all day!" 

Leo’s little dog, Barnaby, has a favorite blue ball. The ball was stuck under the big chair. Leo reached down and gave it to him. Barnaby wagged his tail so fast that his whole body wiggled. Leo realized that helping made everyone feel happy, even the dog.

By the time the sky turned pink and purple, the house felt very peaceful. Leo helped put his favorite books away on the shelf. He put them in a straight line so they were easy to find. He knew that helping at home meant there was more time for stories later.

"Thank you for being such a good helper," his mom said. She gave him a big, warm hug. Leo felt a happy glow inside his heart. 

Now, it was time to rest. Leo felt tired and very sleepy. He climbed into his soft, cozy bed. He pulled the covers up to his chin. He thought about the good work he did today. As he closed his eyes, he knew that his help made the house a wonderful place to be. The world was quiet, the moon was out, and it was time for sweet dreams.
