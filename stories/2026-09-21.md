# The Cozy Garden Friend

**Date:** September 21, 2026  
**Theme:** being kind to animals  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The autumn wind blew softly in the yard. Mia pulled her warm sweater close. She loved this September evening better than any other. The sun was dipping low, painting the sky in sleepy shades of pink and gold.

Mia sat on the porch to read a little book before bed. Just then, she heard a soft rustle near the stone wall. 

"Look," whispered her brother Leo. "A tiny hedgehog!"

Mia wanted to run over and pick it up. But she remembered what Dad always taught them: animals feel safe when we are quiet and gentle. 

"Should we touch it?" Leo asked.

"No," Mia whispered back with a smile. "I would be scared if a giant scooped me up. Kindness means giving him space."

The little hedgehog sniffed the cool air. It was searching for a snug place to sleep through the chilly night. 

"Let us help him keep warm," Mia said. "We can use some dry leaves from our garden."

Together, Mia and Leo moved with slow, careful steps so they would not frighten their new friend. They gathered a gentle pile of golden leaves near a hollow log. They also left a small dish of water by the grass. 

The hedgehog paused. Its dark eyes blinked, sensing no danger at all. Then, with tiny steps, it waddled into the leaves and tucked its nose beneath its paws. It let out a soft sigh, cozy and safe.

"He knows we care," Leo whispered happily.

"Yes," Mia said. "When you show your quiet love, animals can feel it."

The silver moon began to rise, shining down like a nightlight over the yard. 

"Time for bed, my sweet helpers," Mom called gently from the door.

Mia smiled down at the resting hedgehog. Inside her house, her own bed was waiting, fluffy and warm. She climbed under her quilt, feeling peaceful in her heart. Outside, the garden was still, the little creature was asleep, and the whole world was ready for sweet dreams.
