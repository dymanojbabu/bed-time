# Oliver’s Shiny Stones

**Date:** June 12, 2026  
**Theme:** apologizing and making things right  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Oliver was a small bear who loved to play in the deep, green woods. On this warm June evening, he and his friend Pippa the rabbit were sitting together under a big oak tree. Pippa had made a beautiful tower of smooth, gray stones. She worked very hard on it. Each stone was balanced just right. 

Oliver wanted to show Pippa a shiny yellow firefly. He ran over quickly, but his big furry foot bumped the tower. *Crash!* Down went the stones. 

"Oh no," Pippa said, her long ears drooping. 

Oliver felt a heavy feeling in his tummy. What would he do? His mom always told him that when we make a mistake, we must tell our friends we are sorry. 

"I am so sorry, Pippa," Oliver said softly. "I did not mean to knock it down."

Pippa looked up. A gentle apology does help a sad heart feel better. "Thank you, Oliver," she said. 

"I will help you put it back together," Oliver said. He wanted to keep his promise. Before they rebuilt, Oliver used a small twig to write a sweet message in the soft dirt: *SORRY AND BEST FRIENDS*. Then, they worked together to stack the stones again. Some people say that fixing a mistake makes a friendship even stronger. Soon, the tower was tall and strong once more.

They both smiled. The heavy feeling in Oliver’s tummy was gone, replaced by a warm, happy glow. The stars began to twinkle like tiny nightlights in the summer sky. It was time for some rest. Oliver and Pippa curled up in their cozy beds, ready to sleep under the June moon.
