# The Penny Change Game

**Date:** June 29, 2026  
**Theme:** counting coins and making change  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The summer stars **were** shining bright in **the** quiet woods. It was June, and the night air was warm and sweet. Little Mia walked **up** to the cozy treetop stand. It was run by Mrs. Hazel, a **kind** squirrel **who** loved to help her friends. Mrs. Hazel **has** a shop full of sleepy-time treats.

Mia wanted to buy a blue dream-berry. "It is sweeter **than** honey," **she** said. "How much does it cost?"

"It costs four copper pennies," Mrs. Hazel said with a soft smile.

Mia looked in her pocket. She did not have four pennies. Instead, **she** had one shiny silver nickel. "I **know** a nickel is worth five pennies," Mia said. "But **which** coin do I use if I do not have four pennies? **Can** I still buy the berry?"

"Yes, you can!" said Mrs. Hazel. "We can make **these** coins **work** together. Let us count **the** change."

Mia gave her nickel to the squirrel. Mrs. Hazel held up the silver coin. "This is five," she said. Then, she handed one copper penny back to Mia. "We count down. Five minus four leaves one. This penny is your change."

Mia let out a little **laugh**. "So, five is one more than four! Counting is like a fun game."

"You did wonderful **work**," Mrs. Hazel whispered.

Mia held her sweet dream-berry in one hand and her shiny change in the other. **She** felt very proud. 

"**Look** at the moon," Mrs. Hazel said gently. "It is time to rest."

Mia nodded. She ate her sweet berry and snuggled into her warm bed. She put her penny on her nightstand, watching it glow in the moonlight. She closed her eyes, happy and ready for sleep.
