# Tucking in the Toys

**Date:** August 21, 2026  
**Theme:** taking care of books and toys  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The warm August sun began to set, painting the bedroom walls in soft shades of gold and purple. It was time for bed, but Leo’s favorite things were spread out across the carpet. 

His storybooks lay open with bent corners, and colorful wooden blocks were scattered every which way. 

"Our toys worked hard playing with us today," Dad said with a gentle smile. "Let’s help them find their cozy spots so they can sleep too."

Leo nodded. He wanted his things to have a good rest, just like he did. 

First, Leo picked up a picture book about a little red bear. He carefully smoothed down the folded pages. "Books like to be closed flat so their paper stays neat," Leo whispered. He placed the book gently on the shelf, right next to the others. One by one, all his favorite stories were lined up, safe and snug.

Next, Leo looked at those bright wooden cars on the floor. One little car had a smudge of sticky fruit juice on its wheels. Leo took a damp cloth to wash it clean until it was shiny again. Then, he gathered the cars and blocks together into their soft cloth bin. 

"You did a wonderful job caring for your toys," Dad said softly. "Now, whenever you want to play tomorrow, you will know just where to look."

The room felt calm, peaceful, and tidy. Everything was safe in its special home. 

Leo climbed into his own warm bed and pulled the soft quilt up to his chin. He gently hugged his favorite stuffed bear and tucked a tiny blanket over its paws. 

Dad turned off the lamp, leaving only the little nightlight glowing like a sleepy star. 

"Time to go to sleep, little bear," Leo murmured. 

Outside the window, late summer crickets sang a gentle lullaby. With his room cozy and his books and toys tucked in, Leo closed his eyes and drifted happily off to sleep.
