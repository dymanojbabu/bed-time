# The Brave Little Secret

**Date:** May 12, 2026  
**Theme:** being honest even when it is hard  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The sun was setting on a warm May evening. Mia was playing near her mother’s desk. On the desk sat a glass star that sparkled like ice. It felt **cold** in her hands **when** she picked it **up** to look at the light. Mia loved how the star caught the colors of the room.

Suddenly, the star slipped. It fell onto the rug with a soft thud. Mia saw a tiny crack near the edge. Her heart began to beat fast. **What** should she do? She could hide the star behind **those** books on the shelf. She could **keep** the secret all to herself for a **long** **time**. Maybe no one would ever know.

But when Mia looked at the crack, she felt a heavy weight in her tummy. She knew **we** are always happier when we are honest. Mia found **her** mother in the kitchen. 

"Mom?" Mia said softly. "I have something to **tell** you."

Her mother knelt down and looked into her eyes. "I am listening, sweetie. You **can** tell me anything."

"I broke the glass star," Mia said. A small tear fell down her cheek. "I am so sorry."

Her mother gave her a big, warm hug. "Thank you for being brave," she said. "It is hard to say what happened when we make a mistake. But telling the truth is always the right choice. **These** things can often be fixed, but a secret is much harder to carry."

Together, they found **some** glue. They fixed the star and put it back on the desk. The heavy feeling in Mia’s tummy went away. She felt light and happy again. 

Now, it was **time** for bed. The moon was high in the sky, shining through the window. Mia climbed into her soft, cozy blankets. She felt proud of herself for being honest. 

"Goodnight, brave girl," her mother whispered. 

Mia closed her eyes. She was ready to **sleep**. The house was quiet, the star was fixed, and her heart was at peace.
