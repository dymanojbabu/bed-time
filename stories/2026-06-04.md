# Barnaby’s Warm Helping Hands

**Date:** June 04, 2026  
**Theme:** helping at home  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The sun began to set behind the tall pine trees. Barnaby the little bear looked out the window. It had been a long, fun day of play, but now it was time to help. 

"Mama," Barnaby said, "what can I do for you before we go to sleep?"

Mama Bear smiled. She was wiping the table. "You can help me keep our cozy den tidy, my sweet bear," she said.

First, Barnaby helped pick up his wooden blocks. He put them in the toy box. Then, he went to the kitchen. Mama had been busy cooking dinner, and there were warm bowls to clean. Barnaby wanted to help wash them. He stood on a little stool. Together, both of them washed the bowls. The soapy bubbles looked like fluffy white clouds. 

"You are a great helper," Mama Bear said. She gave him a soft towel to dry the cups. Barnaby felt so proud. Helping at home made his heart feel warm and happy. 

After the kitchen was clean, it was time to rest. Outside, the night wind gently blew through the big oak tree. The tree shook its leaves as if to say goodnight. 

"Let’s sit by the window," Mama Bear said. They snuggled together in the big, soft chair. 

Barnaby looked up at the night sky. The silver moon was shining bright, and the first stars started to peek out. Barnaby began to yawn. His little bear eyes felt very heavy. 

Mama Bear began to sing a sweet, quiet song. It was a song about a little bear who did a wonderful job helping his family. 

"Goodnight, my helper," she whispered. Barnaby closed his eyes, ready for happy dreams, knowing his home was clean, safe, and filled with love.
