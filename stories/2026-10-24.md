# The Great Pumpkin Roll

**Date:** October 24, 2026  
**Theme:** teamwork makes things easier  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

On a cool October evening, Benny the bunny had a big chore to finish before bedtime. He wanted to roll a giant, round pumpkin to his cozy hollow before the stars came out. 

Benny pressed his front paws against the smooth orange shell. He pushed and huffed, but the pumpkin did not move very far at all. It was much heavier than he had expected. He sat down in the dry leaves with a tired sigh. 

"Need a helper?" asked a friendly voice. 

Pip the badger took a slow walk down the winding trail. He gave Benny a warm smile. "Look at that lovely pumpkin! Which way do you need to roll it?"

"Down to my front door," Benny replied softly. "I tried my hardest, but it is just too much work for one little bunny."

Pip patted Benny’s shoulder gently. "Two friends are stronger than one. If we push together, it will move easily."

Benny stood back up and placed his paws on the pumpkin. Pip stood right beside him. Together, they counted: "One, two, three, push!"

With four strong paws working as one, the heavy pumpkin rolled right along the soft moss. It moved so quickly and smoothly that Benny could not help but laugh. 

"You were right," Benny said happily. "Working together is the best way to get things done."

In just a few moments, the pumpkin was parked safely beside Benny’s porch. Benny went inside to open his wooden window shutters so the gentle autumn breeze could drift in. Then, the two friends strolled over to the quiet stream to wash their dusty paws in the cool, splashing water. 

The forest was turning a deep, sleepy blue. The silver moon rose high above the tall pine trees. Pip waved goodnight and began his quiet journey home to his own snug burrow. 

Benny climbed into his warm nest of soft blankets and closed his eyes. His heart felt light and happy. The peaceful night tucked him in, and he drifted off into sweet, restful dreams.
