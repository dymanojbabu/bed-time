# Barnaby’s Cozy Nest

**Date:** September 15, 2026  
**Theme:** finishing what you start  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

*Once* upon a cool September evening, a little dormouse named Barnaby decided to build a special dream nest. The autumn wind was blowing soft and sweet through the tall grass outside.

Barnaby carried five golden leaves *into* his hollow log. Next, his mother *gave* him some fluffy dandelion seeds. "These *will* keep you warm tonight," she whispered. 

Barnaby began to weave the stems together. He tucked them all *around* the edges of his bed. At first, it was fun. But weaving was a *long* task for little paws. Barnaby stopped. His nest was only half-finished. One side was thick and soft, but the other side was bare and drafty.

"I think I want to stop now," Barnaby said with a tiny yawn. "It is taking longer *than* I thought, and I just want to rest."

His mother sat beside him on the moss. "It is easy to stop halfway *because* your paws feel tired," she said gently. "But if you finish the job, you can sleep with a happy heart. Let us add just a few *more* leaves together."

Barnaby nodded. He reached out to *get* another golden leaf. Working side by side, *they* tucked each stem neatly into place. Barnaby pressed soft moss into every corner. With every little piece he tucked in, the nest grew snugger and warmer.

*Then*, Barnaby stepped back. He looked at what he had *made*. The nest was completely finished! It was round, deep, and beautifully soft. A proud, cozy feeling filled his little chest. Finishing his work felt even better *than* starting it.

"Now it is truly ready," his mother smiled. 

*Both* of them curled up inside the circle of leaves. Barnaby pulled a smooth green petal up *to* his chin like a blanket. Outside, the crickets began to hum their slow bedtime song. The silver moon rose high in the sky, watchfully quiet. Barnaby closed his sleepy eyes, safe and peaceful, drifting happily off to sleep.
