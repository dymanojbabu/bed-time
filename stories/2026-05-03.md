# Oliver’s Yellow Gift

**Date:** May 03, 2026  
**Theme:** giving to others who need help  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Oliver the Otter lived by a sparkling stream. It was the third day of May, and the evening light was golden and soft. Oliver liked to keep his home very neat. Before bed, he would always wash his face and his paws in the cool, clear water.

Oliver had a special basket filled with soft, yellow yarn. He loved to sit on his porch and feel the fuzzy strings between his toes. He wanted to use the yarn to make a big, thick rug for his floor. Just then, he saw his friend, Pip the Bird. Pip was flying low to the ground. She was trying to find some feathers or dry grass, but the ground was bare.

"Hello, Pip," Oliver called out. "What will you do tonight? It is going to be a chilly night."

Pip landed softly on a mossy stone. "I do not know," she said with a tiny sigh. "I need to make my nest warm for my family, but I cannot find anything soft in the woods. I wonder how we will stay warm."

Oliver looked at his basket of yellow yarn. He knew the yarn would make his own floor look very pretty. But Pip was his friend, and she was in need. He looked at the yarn, and then he looked at Pip. Helping a friend felt much more important than a new rug.

"I can help!" Oliver said. He picked up the yarn with his paws. He gave Pip a very large bundle of the soft, yellow fuzz. "This has plenty of warmth for your whole family," he said.

Pip was so happy that she sang a little song of thanks. She flew to her tree to finish her nest. Oliver watched her go. He did not have enough yarn for a rug anymore, but he did not mind. His heart felt full and happy. 

The moon rose high in the sky. Oliver went inside his cozy home. He felt peaceful knowing his friend was safe and warm. He closed his eyes and listened to the soft wind, ready for a night of sweet dreams.
