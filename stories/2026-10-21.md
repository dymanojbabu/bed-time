# Clara’s Thank-You Walk

**Date:** October 21, 2026  
**Theme:** gratitude and saying thank you  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

On a crisp October evening, little Clara walked down the lane with her mother. The autumn air was cool, and the golden trees began to whisper in the breeze. Clara liked to sing a quiet tune as they strolled along the way.

Under an old oak tree, Clara stopped. She found a shiny, smooth acorn resting in the moss. "Look at this one!" she cheered softly.

A friendly neighbor waved from his porch and offered them some sweet apple cider. "Thank you very much," Clara said. Saying thank you made her heart feel warm, like sunshine on a chilly day.

As they walked further, Clara watched the gentle town. People were heading home to their families, turning on soft yellow lamps in their windows. The street felt peaceful and calm. 

"Mom, look at the sky," Clara whispered. The first silver stars were twinkling above the rooftops. Clara smiled. She was glad she could see them shine so bright.

"Why are you smiling so big, Clara?" her mother asked with a gentle laugh.

"I am thinking of all my favorite things," Clara answered happily. "I love my warm mittens. I love our walk. And I love saying thank you."

"Saying thank you is a wonderful gift," her mother replied. "It reminds us of all the goodness we share."

Soon, they reached their blue front door. The porch light cast a gentle glow on the steps. 

"Come inside, sweetheart," her mother whispered. "It is time to rest."

Inside, the house smelled of cinnamon and sweet dreams. Clara put on her soft pajamas and climbed into bed. She pulled the fluffy quilt up to her chin. 

Before closing her eyes, Clara made a quiet list of things she loved. She thanked the moon for its light, her bed for its softness, and her family for their hugs. With a thankful heart and a sleepy smile, Clara drifted off into sweet, peaceful dreams.
