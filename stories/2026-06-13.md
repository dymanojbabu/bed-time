# Pippin’s Warm Gift

**Date:** June 13, 2026  
**Theme:** giving to others who need help  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The summer sky on this lovely June night was turning a soft, warm pink. Pippin, a **small** mouse, sat on a tree branch. He looked at his pile of **yellow** flowers and cozy green leaves. He loved his nest. It was the perfect home. 

Suddenly, Pippin heard a soft **call** from the bushes below. It was his friend, Barnaby the bluebird. Barnaby looked very sad. A big gust of wind had blown his nest away. 

"I do not know **how** to make a new bed before dark," Barnaby sighed. "I am **so** tired."

Pippin looked at his own pile of soft, fluffy moss. He wanted to help his friend. Pippin knew he had **many** nice things, and sharing them would help Barnaby feel safe. 

"Do not worry," Pippin said with a smile. "**Come** with me. **We** can make you a new bed together."

The two friends began to **walk** **around** the big oak tree. Pippin went **into** his home and brought out his softest yellow leaves. He gave them to Barnaby. "These will keep you warm," Pippin said. 

Next, they went to the sparkly stream. Pippin helped Barnaby **wash** some smooth pebbles to make the base of the nest strong. 

"You are so kind to help me," Barnaby chirped. "This helps **us** both feel happy."

Soon, the new nest was ready. It was tucked safely in the branches. It was soft, warm, and perfect. 

"Thank you, Pippin," Barnaby said, snuggling down. "Now I can **go** to sleep."

Pippin waved goodnight and climbed back to his own bed. He did not mind having a little less moss, because his heart felt full and happy. Giving to his friend was the best feeling of all. 

The silver moon began to shine. Pippin crawled under his leaf blanket. The forest was quiet **now**. It was time for Pippin, Barnaby, and you to close your eyes and dream sweet, happy dreams.
