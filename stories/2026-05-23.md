# Lily’s Little Helper Hands

**Date:** May 23, 2026  
**Theme:** helping at home  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The sun was going down on a warm May evening, painting the sky in soft pink and purple. Lily looked **around** her bedroom. Her colorful toys were still scattered on the rug. "I **know** what to do," Lily whispered to herself. She wanted to help **her** mom **before** it was time for sleep. 

Lily put her wooden blocks into their blue box, one **by** one. She placed her soft plush rabbit neatly on the pillow. "**Look** how tidy the room is now!" Lily said. It felt **very** cozy and ready for dreams. 

Next, Lily walked into the living room. Her mom was folding the clean laundry. The evening breeze coming through the window was getting a little **cold**, so Lily picked up a soft green blanket from the chair. She carefully folded it and placed it on the sofa. 

"Thank you, Lily," her mom said with a sweet smile. "You are such a big help. When **we** work together, our home feels so happy." Lily felt a warm, happy glow inside. Helping was **some** of her favorite helper work. 

"Now," mom said, "come **sit** right here next to me." They snuggled together on the soft sofa. Lily reached for the bookshelf. "I found **my** favorite book," she said. "Can we **read** this one tonight?" 

Mom opened the book and began to tell the story. Lily listened to **its** gentle, soothing words. She knew **that** she had done a wonderful job helping her family today. 

As the silver stars began to twinkle outside the window, Lily’s eyes grew heavy. She felt safe, warm, and loved. Her quiet home was ready for sleep, and so was she. "Goodnight, sweet home," Lily whispered, drifting off into a beautiful, peaceful dream.
