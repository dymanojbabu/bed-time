# Pip’s Stargazing Nest

**Date:** July 02, 2026  
**Theme:** setting a goal and working toward it  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Pip the little hedgehog had a big goal. He wanted to build a cozy stargazing nest on top of the high hill. He wanted to finish it before the warm summer week was over. It was a big task for a tiny hedgehog. But Pip knew he could do it if he worked a little bit every day. 

On the first morning, Pip started his climb. It was a long way up the grassy hill. He carried three big oak leaves with him. He had to decide which path to take. He chose the smooth path with the pretty yellow flowers. His forest friends were busy playing tag in the meadow. They asked Pip to play, but Pip smiled and shook his head. "I want to make my cozy nest first," he said. 

The next afternoon, Pip gathered soft green moss. He walked slow and steady, taking one small step at a time. Each tiny step brought him closer to his goal. Then, on the third afternoon, he found fluffy dandelion seeds to use as a pillow. He worked patiently, never giving up. 

By evening, the nest was finally finished. Pip went down to the cool stream to wash his dusty little paws. "Look!" chirped a happy bluebird who watched from a tree branch. "You worked so hard, and now your dream has come true all because you did not give up!" 

Pip climbed up the hill one last time. He lay down in his warm, soft nest. He watched the deep blue night sky open up above him. The round moon showed its gentle, silver light. Pip felt so proud of his hard work. He let out a soft sigh, closed his eyes, and drifted off into sweet, happy dreams.
