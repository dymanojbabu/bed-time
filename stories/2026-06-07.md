# Pip’s Sparkling Sleepytime

**Date:** June 07, 2026  
**Theme:** brushing teeth and healthy habits  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The warm summer sun **had** set, **and** the shiny stars began to blink. Pip the little squirrel sat on a tall oak branch. It was a **long**, busy **day** of playing with his friends. Now, the forest was quiet, and it was time for sleep. 

Pip skipped inside his cozy tree home. His mother smiled **and** handed him his favorite blue toothbrush. "Do you **know** **what** we do next to get ready for bed?" she asked in a soft whisper. 

"Brush our teeth!" Pip cheered. 

They stood **together** by the warm sink. Pip put a tiny drop of sweet, minty paste on his brush. He brushed **each** little tooth, up and down, round and round, just like his mother showed him. He wanted to do his **best** to keep his smile bright and healthy. It felt cool, fresh, and clean **in** his mouth. 

Next, Pip washed his furry paws and his face with warm water. "Good habits help us sleep well," his mother would **tell** him. Pip loved **her** gentle bedtime words. Pip even liked to **write** a little checkmark on his chart to show he finished his healthy routines. He felt so proud. 

Outside, the sleepy night wind began to blow. Pip could hear the owls **call** out to one another. The little birds were already tucked away in **their** cozy nests. 

Pip climbed into his warm bed. He pulled his leaf blanket up to his chin. His teeth were clean, his paws were clean, and his mind was happy. He knew he would wake up feeling strong and ready for a new day. 

"Goodnight, world," Pip whispered. He closed his eyes and drifted off to sleep.
