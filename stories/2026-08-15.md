# Pip’s Stargazing Nest

**Date:** August 15, 2026  
**Theme:** setting a goal and working toward it  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Pip was a small mouse with big ideas. One warm August night, he looked up at the twinkling sky and made a special plan. He wanted to build the coziest stargazing bed on top of Willow Hill. 

"It will take time," he told the moon, "but I can get it done if I work a little bit every day."

Setting a goal made Pip feel excited. Each morning, he chose one small job to do. On Monday, he walked to the meadow and found ten soft, green leaves. On Tuesday, he visited his friend Mia the rabbit to see if he could borrow some fresh moss. Mia smiled and shared her softest patches.

"Will you call me when it is ready?" her gentle voice asked.

"I will!" Pip promised. He showed her how high the little nest was growing. 

By Thursday, many forest animals were stopping by to watch him build. Pip worked slowly and carefully. He placed each dry twig in a circle so the bed would keep out any cold nighttime breeze. The nest even had its own pillow made of fluffy dandelion seeds. 

Sometimes Pip felt tired, but he would look up at the top of the hill and remember his goal. He knew that big things take patience, and every small step brought him closer. 

On Friday night, the project was finished. Pip climbed in and rested his paws. The nest has everything a tired little mouse could wish for. It was soft, warm, and smelled like sweet pine needles. 

The silver moon rose, shining down on Willow Hill. The crickets began to sing a slow, sleepy song. Pip curled his tail around his toes and took a deep, happy breath. He closed his eyes, feeling very proud of his hard work, and floated off to sleep under the quiet August stars.
