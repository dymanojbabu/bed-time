# The Thank-You Jar

**Date:** August 13, 2026  
**Theme:** gratitude and saying thank you  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The summer sky was turning a soft, deep blue on a warm August evening. Leo sat on the front porch with his grandpa. The crickets chirped a slow, sleepy tune in the grass. 

"Grandpa," Leo asked, "**what** are the best treasures **in** the whole world?"

Grandpa smiled a **very** gentle smile. "Well, Leo, the best things are **not** things you can **buy** at a store. They are the little gifts we see every single day."

Grandpa handed Leo a smooth glass jar. "Let us **put** our favorite moments from today inside **your** jar **before** you go to sleep."

Leo looked at the empty jar. "How do we put moments in here?"

"Just whisper a quiet thank-you for what made you happy," Grandpa said. "**Open** the lid, whisper your thank-you, and close it tight."

Leo thought for a moment. He remembered the sweet, cold watermelon he ate at lunch. It was juicier **than** any fruit he had ever tasted. He took off the lid. "Thank you for the sweet melon," he whispered. 

Next, Leo remembered running **fast** through the yard with his puppy. "Thank you for playful friends," he whispered softly into the jar.

Then, he thought of how Grandpa was **always** there to give the biggest, warmest hugs. Leo smiled. "Thank you for cozy bedtime stories."

"Look," Grandpa whispered. Leo held up the jar. In the pale moonlight, it seemed to glow with a warm, quiet light. 

"When you say thank you, you will notice you have **more** happy moments **than** you ever knew," Grandpa said. "Gratitude makes your heart feel **so** light and peaceful."

"Are **these** memories mine to keep?" Leo asked, rubbing his sleepy eyes.

"They will **always** be in your heart," Grandpa said with a wink.

Leo set the jar gently on his nightstand and climbed into bed. He pulled the soft blanket up to his chin. With a grateful heart, Leo closed his eyes and drifted into sweet, happy dreams.
