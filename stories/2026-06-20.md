# Leo and the Shadow Monster

**Date:** June 20, 2026  
**Theme:** being brave when scared  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Leo lay in his cozy bed on a warm summer night. Outside, the crickets played a soft song. But inside, Leo was awake. When the sun goes down, the bedroom gets very dark. Leo looked at his bedroom wall and saw a big, strange shape. It looked like a giant monster with long, waving arms! He felt his heart beat a little fast, but he decided to be brave.

Leo reached out and turned on his bedtime lamp. A warm, yellow light filled the room, making the dark corners go away. He reached for his favorite plush puppy. "Let us sit together," Leo whispered to the puppy. He pulled the soft toy close to his chest. 

With the bright light on, Leo looked at the wall again. He found that the giant monster was not a monster at all. It was just his favorite green sweater hanging on the back of his chair! Its long, scary arms were just the sleeves moving in the gentle breeze from the window. Leo laughed a quiet, happy laugh.

"You can use your eyes and your mind to be brave," Leo whispered to his puppy. "When you feel scared, you can just turn on the light and see the truth."

Leo felt very proud of himself. He had faced his fear and found there was nothing to worry about. He turned off the lamp so the room was dark and peaceful once more. The dark did not feel scary now. It felt soft and quiet, like a big, cozy hug. 

Leo closed his eyes, ready to start to sleep. He and his puppy snuggled together under the warm sheets. With a deep, slow breath, Leo let his mind drift away into sweet, happy dreams.
