# Bella’s Sparkly Star Sleep

**Date:** June 28, 2026  
**Theme:** brushing teeth and healthy habits  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Bella was a little bunny who loved warm June nights. Tonight, the stars *were* bright in the dark sky. It was time *to* get ready for bed, but Bella wanted to keep playing outside. "Time to wash your paws and brush your teeth," her mother said with a gentle smile. 

Bella let out a soft sigh. "Do I have to do it tonight?" *she* asked, looking at the glowing moon. 

Mother Bunny sat *down* on the cozy bathroom rug. "We do *these* things *because* they help our bodies rest," she explained. "We want to *make* our teeth clean and bright, just like the stars. *Look* in the mirror. Can you see where the sweet summer berries we ate for dinner are hiding?"

Bella hopped *to* the sink. She picked up her toothbrush, *which* was sparkly green. Her mother showed her a special *way* to brush. "We brush up and down, round and round," they sang together. *They* made *many* white bubbles that looked like fluffy clouds. Bella liked to *sit* on her wooden stool and brush for two whole minutes. It felt like a fun game to play before bed. 

Next, Bella washed her soft face. She used *some* warm water to rinse away the dirt from her busy day of hopping in the grass. Her face felt fresh, clean, and warm. 

"Now my teeth are shiny, and my body feels so ready for bed," Bella said, yawning a big bunny yawn. 

"That is what we *call* healthy habits," her mother whispered, tucking her into her soft, leafy bed. "They keep our bodies strong and help us have the sweetest dreams."

As the gentle June wind blew through the trees, Bella closed her eyes. Her mouth felt clean, her body felt relaxed, and she drifted off to sleep, dreaming of sparkly stars.
