# The Team of Tiny Helpers

**Date:** May 16, 2026  
**Theme:** helping at home  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Maya looked at the clock. It was a golden afternoon on May 16, and the sun was shining through the window. She saw her mom sitting on the sofa, resting her eyes for just a moment. Maya wanted to do something kind. She found her little brother, Leo, playing with his red trucks on the rug.

"Leo, let us do our part to help today," Maya whispered.

Leo nodded and stood up. Together, they walked into the kitchen. Maya knew just what to do. "First, we should wash the red apples for a snack," she said. They moved their small stools right up to the sink. Maya let the cool water run over the fruit until it was shiny and clean. Leo helped wipe off the extra water with a soft, dry cloth.

"Now, let us look for the best snack bowls," Maya said. She looked in the cupboard under the counter. She found two blue bowls and one green one. Maya put the apples inside. It looked so pretty.

Leo wanted to help even more. He took a small towel and wiped the crumbs off the table. He worked so hard to make sure the kitchen was tidy. "We make a great team," Maya said. "We are the best helpers in the world!"

When Mom woke up from her short nap, she walked into the kitchen. A big smile grew on her face. "Look at this!" she cried. "You did so much to help us get ready for the evening."

Maya felt a warm glow in her heart. Helping at home made the whole house feel happy and calm. As the sun began to set, the sky turned a soft, sleepy purple. They all sat down to eat their snack together.

After the apples were gone, it was time for bed. Maya and Leo put their bowls in the sink. They knew that when everyone does a little bit, it makes the day better. Maya climbed into her cozy bed, feeling peaceful and proud. The house was quiet, the work was done, and it was time for sweet dreams.
