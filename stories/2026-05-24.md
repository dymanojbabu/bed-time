# Barnaby’s Purple Soup

**Date:** May 24, 2026  
**Theme:** trying new foods  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Barnaby Bear sat at the cozy kitchen table on a warm May evening. The stars were just starting to blink outside the window. Barnaby was very hungry, but when his mom placed a bowl of bright purple soup on the table, he stopped. 

"What is this?" Barnaby asked, pointing his little paw out. 

"It is purple clover soup," Mama Bear said with a soft smile. "I made it because the beautiful spring flowers are in bloom."

Barnaby did not want to eat the soup. It did not look like his favorite yellow honey-bread. It was a new color, and new things could be scary. 

"Just take a tiny taste," Mama said gently. "You can try some on the very tip of your spoon. Trying new foods is part of growing big and strong."

Barnaby looked down at the purple bowl. Who knew what clover tasted like? He closed both of his eyes. Then, he took a tiny, little sip. It did not taste scary at all. It tasted sweet, like warm forest sunshine and fresh morning rain. 

"Oh!" Barnaby said, letting out a happy little laugh. "It is actually very yummy!"

"See?" Mama said, wiping a purple drop from his chin. "Sometimes those new foods can surprise us if we just give them a chance."

Mama rubbed Barnaby's fluffy head. "I will always help you find good things to eat, my sweet little bear."

Barnaby happily ate each spoonful of the warm soup until his bowl was empty. He felt his tummy grow full, warm, and very cozy. He wondered how he had ever been worried to try it in the first place. 

Now, it was time for sleep. Barnaby snuggled deep into his soft, warm bed. He closed his eyes, happy that he had tried something new. He slowly drifted off to sleep, dreaming of beautiful purple fields under the silver moon.
