# Lily’s Thank You Garden

**Date:** April 26, 2026  
**Theme:** gratitude and saying thank you  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The sun **was** starting to hide behind the big, green hills. Lily **and** her Papa sat on the soft grass. It was April 26, a warm and very quiet night. Papa smiled at Lily. "Before **we** go to sleep," he said, "let's find things to be thankful for."

Lily looked **up** at the pink and orange clouds. "I **wish** I **can** tell the sun thank you for the bright light," she said. She watched **two** blue birds fly home to their nest in the tall tree. Papa told **her** that saying thank you **was** like giving a gift to the world. It makes the person saying it feel good, too.

"**Look** at the garden," Papa said. "**Does** it **not** look pretty tonight?" Lily nodded. She loved the way the white flowers smelled in the cool air. She began to **laugh** when a small, brown toad hopped over her shoe. "Thank you, little toad, for visiting us," she whispered. The toad blinked its eyes and hopped away into the bushes.

Lily thought about **all** her friends at school. She **always** felt happy when they played games on the grass during recess. "Thank you for my friends," she said softly. Papa smiled and patted her hand. "When you close **your** eyes at night, you can think of all the good parts of **your** day. It helps you have sweet dreams."

The stars began to blink in the dark sky. Lily felt very sleepy. She knew that even when things are small, we can still say thank you. It made her heart feel warm, like a cozy blanket. "I am thankful for you, Papa," she said. Papa gave her a gentle hug. "And I am thankful for you, Lily."

Now it was time to rest. The world was quiet **and** still. Lily felt safe and happy as she drifted off to sleep, dreaming of all the wonderful things she would see tomorrow.
