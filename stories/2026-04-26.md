# Maya’s Sparkling Smile

**Date:** April 26, 2026  
**Theme:** brushing teeth and healthy habits  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The moon was high in the sky on this quiet night of April 26. It had been a wonderful **day** of playing in the park and running under the warm sun. Now, the sun was gone, and it was **time** for sleep. Maya stood **up** from her soft chair and began her nightly **walk** to the bathroom. She and her dad went **together** to get ready for bed.


"Do you know **what** we do next?" Dad asked with a warm smile.

Maya laughed. She knew exactly what to do. She reached for her favorite bright blue toothbrush. She liked to **use** the one that had little yellow stars on the handle. She **made** sure to put just a tiny bit of minty toothpaste on the bristles, just like a small, green pea.

Maya started to brush. She brushed **each** tooth, one by one, in little circles. She brushed the ones in the front and the ones tucked way in the back where she chewed her dinner. She wanted to **see** them sparkle in the mirror. As she brushed, she thought about how important it was to keep her teeth strong and white. Brushing helps keep the sticky sugar bugs **off** so her smile stays bright.

"Good job," Dad said. "Healthy habits are important for everyone. Strong teeth help **people** eat crunchy apples and smile big for photos."

Maya rinsed her mouth with cool, clear water. Her mouth felt fresh and very clean. Healthy habits were easy when you did **them** every single night. When she was finished, the light **goes** out in the bathroom.

Maya hopped into her cozy bed and pulled the soft blankets **up** to her chin. Now it was time to **read** a short story about a sleepy owl in the forest. As she listened to the words, her eyes grew heavy. She felt happy knowing her body was healthy and her teeth were clean. She closed her eyes, ready to dream of tomorrow’s adventures. Goodnight, Maya.
