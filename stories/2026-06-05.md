# Penny the Piggy Bank's Quiet Song

**Date:** June 05, 2026  
**Theme:** saving money and piggy banks  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

It was a warm June night. The stars **were** just starting to blink outside Lily's window. Lily sat on **her** soft bed, looking at three shiny silver coins. Her grandfather **gave** them to her that morning for helping in the garden. "What **will** you do with them?" her mother asked. Lily wanted to spend them right away on a tiny toy. But then she looked at her **small**, pink piggy bank sitting on the shelf. Its name was Penny.

"I **know** it is hard to wait," her mother **said** with a warm smile. She helped Lily **write** a wish on a little piece of paper: *A green drawing notebook.* "Many **people** save their coins to buy special things," her mother explained. "**First**, you put them **in**. Then, you let them grow."

Lily liked that idea. She took the three coins **out** of her pocket. With a gentle *clink, clink, clink*, she slipped them into Penny's back. Lily **had** a few other coins in there from **before**. Now, the piggy bank felt a little heavier. Penny did not look hungry anymore; she looked happy. Lily knew her money was safe inside, sleeping and waiting for the perfect day.

The bedroom was very quiet now. Lily tucked her favorite blanket up to her chin. It was time to **sleep**. She looked at her piggy bank one last time in the soft moonlight. Saving money felt like planting a little seed that would grow into something wonderful. She closed her eyes, ready for sweet dreams, while Penny kept her treasures safe all through the night.
