# Leo’s Bright Bedtime Sparkle

**Date:** April 29, 2026  
**Theme:** brushing teeth and healthy habits  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Leo lived in a cozy house where the stars always seemed to twinkle just for him. It was the night of April 29, and the air outside was soft and warm. After a long day of playing tag and drawing pictures, Leo felt his eyes getting a little bit heavy. He knew it was time to get ready for his dreams, but he had one very important job to do first.

Leo walked into the bathroom and turned on the tap. The water felt nice as it splashed into the sink. It was time to use his favorite tool: his bright green toothbrush. Leo put a small dot of minty paste on the bristles. He looked at his smile in the mirror. They were his very own teeth, and he wanted to keep them strong.

"I have to make sure I get every single spot," Leo whispered to himself. He moved the brush from left to right in small, gentle circles. He brushed the top teeth, then he brushed the bottom ones. He did not forget those tricky teeth in the very back that do all the hard work of chewing. He brushed for two whole minutes while he hummed a happy little tune.

Once he was finished, he rinsed his mouth with cool water and spit it out. His mouth felt fresh and clean. Leo knew he could tell his friends at school tomorrow how good it feels to have a healthy smile. His mom came in to give him a hug. 

"You can buy a new toy at the store," she said softly, "but you have to take care of the teeth you have so they stay strong forever." 

Leo nodded as he climbed out of his slippers and hopped into his snuggly bed. He felt proud of his healthy habits. The moonlight shone from the window, making his room look magical. He pulled his blankets up high. He felt peaceful and happy. Leo closed his eyes, took a deep breath, and fell fast asleep, dreaming of big adventures.
