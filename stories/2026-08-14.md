# The Cozy Moss Bed

**Date:** August 14, 2026  
**Theme:** teamwork makes things easier  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The golden sun was setting behind the rolling green hills. It had been a long, sunny day, and little Mia the mouse was ready to sleep. She wanted to build a soft bed under the old willow tree.

Mia found many colorful leaves and one big, fluffy puff of cotton. But the puff was very heavy. She pushed with all her might, but it would not move. "How does a little mouse carry such a big treasure?" she wondered. Her tiny paws were tired. 

Just then, her friend Barnaby Bunny hopped over. "I can tell you need some help," Barnaby said with a cheerful smile. "Do you know that sharing the work makes everything easier?"

"I would love some help," said Mia.

"Let us count to three, and then we will start," Barnaby said. "One, two, three, lift!"

Mia held one side, and Barnaby held the other. Together, with their paws working as a team, the big puff felt light as air. What was too hard for one mouse was easy and fun for two friends. They rolled it across the soft moss and tucked it into Mia’s cozy nest. 

Now, the nest was complete. It was filled with many sweet-smelling blossoms and soft white cotton to keep them warm. 

The silver moon began to glow, and tiny fireflies lit up the grass. Cool evening breezes swept through the quiet forest. 

"You may share my nest tonight," Mia whispered softly to Barnaby. 

"Thank you," Barnaby yawned. 

The bright stars are shining high in the night sky now. Mia and Barnaby curled up together in their soft bed, happy that they had worked side by side. As the forest grew still and dark, both little friends closed their eyes and drifted into sweet, peaceful dreams.
