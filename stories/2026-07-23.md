# Oliver and the Summer Breeze

**Date:** July 23, 2026  
**Theme:** being brave when scared  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

On a warm night, Oliver the little bunny lay in his bed. The stars were shining, but Oliver could not sleep. Then he saw a tall shadow dancing on his wall.

Oliver felt his heart beat fast. *What would mom do?* he thought. He remembered her sweet words. "I know you can be brave," she had said. "Take a deep breath to keep calm."

Oliver took a slow breath. "I have my courage," he whispered to the dark.

He sat up and looked around his cozy room. There was no monster under the bed. Still, he wanted to find out what was making that tall shadow. On his way to the window, he held his favorite soft blanket tight. 

When Oliver peeked out the window, he had to laugh softly.

It was just a big oak tree in the yard! The summer wind was moving the branches around. The bright moon made the leaves make silly dancing shadows on his wall. There was nothing to worry about at all. 

Oliver felt very brave. Being brave did not mean he was never scared. It just meant he was strong enough to look closer. 

He stepped back into his warm bed and pulled his sweet blanket up to his chin. The rustling leaves did not sound scary anymore. Now, they sounded like a soft song telling him it was time to rest.

"You are safe, little bunny," Oliver whispered to himself.

He closed his eyes, listened to the peaceful wind, and drifted off into a lovely bedtime dream.
