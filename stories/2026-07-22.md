# A Restful Night for Books and Toys

**Date:** July 22, 2026  
**Theme:** taking care of books and toys  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The warm summer sun was going down, and soft stars began to glow. "It is time to sleep now," said Mom from the doorway. Leo looked around his bedroom. It had been a long, fun day of play. His colorful blocks were scattered on the rug, and his favorite picture book lay open on the floor.

Leo picked up his soft teddy bear. "Our special friends are tired," Leo whispered to Bear. "They worked hard all day."

"They need a safe place to rest, too," Mom said with a gentle smile. "When we take good care of our toys and books, they stay happy and whole for more fun tomorrow."

Leo nodded softly. He picked up the storybook which had spent the afternoon on the rug. He smoothed its paper pages gently with his hand and placed it on the shelf next to the other stories. "Goodnight, book," he whispered. 

Next, Leo found his little red race car, who was resting under the desk. He tucked the car in its cozy box, right alongside his wooden blocks. 

Leo and Mom worked together to put everything in its proper home. "We call this bedtime for the room," Mom said softly. When all the toys were safe, the bedroom felt calm, quiet, and neat. There was no more clutter on the rug—just soft pillows and warm blankets.

Leo climbed into bed and pulled his blanket up to his chin. He felt happy knowing his books and toys were safe, ready to sleep through the warm night.

"Goodnight, room," Leo whispered. 

Outside, the summer wind blew softly through the green trees. The books rested on their shelves, and the toys rested in their boxes. All was quiet, and soon, Leo closed his eyes and drifted off to sleep.
