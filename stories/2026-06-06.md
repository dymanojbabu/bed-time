# Toby’s Tiny Toy Boat

**Date:** June 06, 2026  
**Theme:** finishing what you start  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Toby was a little beaver who loved to build. One warm summer evening, he sat by the stream. The water was cold, but Toby did not mind. He had a grand plan. He wanted to make a tiny toy boat from twigs and leaves. 

He gathered some soft moss and small sticks. His mother watched him work. Toby started to tie the sticks together, but soon, he saw a glowing firefly. He wanted to chase it. "I will finish my boat tomorrow," Toby said with a yawn. 

His mother smiled and said, "It is better to finish what you start, Toby. That way, your project is ready for its big swim before the night is done."

Toby looked at his half-made boat. He decided to try more than once to get it right. He picked up a shiny leaf, which would be the sail. He put each piece in place with care. He used his paws to get the last twig on top. He worked slowly, enjoying the quiet forest sounds. 

At last, the little boat was finished! It was strong and beautiful. Toby gently pushed it into the water. The boat floated softly down the stream. Toby felt so proud. It felt much better to see it float than to leave it unfinished. 

The forest became very quiet. The birds flew back to their trees. The stars began to shine. Toby crawled into his cozy bed of grass. He was happy that he had finished his boat. He closed his eyes and drifted off to sleep, dreaming of new adventures on the river.
