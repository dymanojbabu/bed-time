# Leo and the Blue Pot

**Date:** May 18, 2026  
**Theme:** being honest even when it is hard  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

It was a warm evening on the eighteenth of May. Leo the fox was playing in the garden while the sun began to set. He liked to run fast through the tall green grass. "Watch **what** I can do!" he **said** to his shadow. He kicked his bright red ball high into the air.

Oh no! The ball hit his mom’s favorite blue flower pot. The pot fell over with a loud thud. **Its** handle broke off into **some** small pieces on the patio. Leo felt his heart beat fast. He did not know **what** to do next. Should he hide the pieces under a bush? He looked at the mess and thought **about** the broken pot. He did not **like** the heavy feeling in his tummy.

Leo knew he had to **find** his mom. He slowly walked into the cozy house. He saw her sitting in the big chair by the lamp. She was trying to **read** a storybook. Leo took a deep breath. **First**, he had to be brave. He knew **which** thing was the right thing to do.

"Mom?" Leo whispered. **His** voice was very small. "I have something to **tell** you. I was playing too fast and I broke your blue pot."

Leo felt worried. He wondered if she would be mad. But Mom put her book down and smiled softly. She gave him a big, warm hug.

"Thank you for telling me the truth," she **said**. "Being honest is not always easy. It takes a lot of courage to let the truth **out**."

She told him that when we are honest, it helps **us** feel peaceful inside. Leo felt **so** much better. It was **like** a heavy cloud had blown away from his heart. They went back outside together. Mom showed Leo how to use a little glue to fix the handle.

The silver moon began to shine over the quiet garden. Leo was sleepy after his big day. He knew that being honest was always the best choice. He climbed into his soft, warm bed and closed his eyes. The stars blinked in the dark sky as he fell into a deep, happy sleep.
