# The Shiny Coin Shop

**Date:** August 30, 2026  
**Theme:** counting coins and making change  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The warm summer sun was going down behind the tall green trees. Leo sat on the porch with his little wooden box. It was his favorite time of day. He loved to count the shiny coins he and his sister had saved in our shared glass jar. 

"Let us make a pretend fruit shop before bed," his sister Mia said with a soft laugh. She set out three pretend blue plums. "How much for one plum?"

"One plum is ten cents," Leo smiled. 

Mia handed him a shiny silver quarter. "I only have twenty-five cents," she said. 

Leo knew ten was less than twenty-five, so he needed to give her change. He looked inside his wooden box to see which shiny coins he could use. He did not count fast; he took his time. 

"Ten plus ten is twenty," Leo whispered softly. "And five more makes twenty-five." 

He made a neat little pile with one dime and one nickel. "Ten plus five is fifteen cents. Here is your change, Mia!"

"You are the best shopkeeper," Mia cheered quietly. "Now we have to put those pennies to rest, too."

Together, they counted five bright copper pennies. One, two, three, four, five. They laid the coins down in neat, tidy rows. The gentle *clink, clink, clink* of the metal sounded just like a quiet bedtime song. Counting coins was such fun, but the stars were beginning to twinkle in the late August sky. 

Leo dropped the coins into his jar and closed the lid. His eyes felt very heavy now. He climbed into his warm, cozy bed and pulled the soft blanket up to his chin. The house was quiet and peaceful. Leo closed his eyes, ready to count happy dreams and drift gently off to sleep.
