# The Toys' Bedtime

**Date:** October 02, 2026  
**Theme:** taking care of books and toys  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Leo sat on his cozy rug as an October breeze tapped against the window. He looked around his bedroom. On the floor lay his favorite storybook with its cover bent. Beside him, a little yellow truck rested upside down under the desk. 

Leo’s sister, Maya, stepped inside and sat near him. She picked up the book and smoothed her hand across the colorful pages. 

"Books and toys work hard all day," she said with a gentle laugh. "When we play, they give us wonderful adventures. But when night falls, they need rest, too."

"How does a toy sleep?" Leo asked, tilting his head. 

"Just like you," Maya smiled. "People can buy new toys at a store, but they cannot buy the happy memories we share with them. We take care of our things because they are our helpers and friends."

Leo nodded softly. Now he understood. 

He picked up his little yellow truck and placed it gently on the low shelf. He wrapped a soft cloth around its wheels so it felt cozy. "Sleep well, truck," he whispered. 

Next, Leo reached for the book. Carefully, he smoothed down the folded corners. He closed the book gently so the pages would stay clean and neat. Tomorrow, he knew he can open it again to see the brave knight and the friendly dragon. 

Together, Leo and Maya gathered the colorful blocks and placed them in their wooden box. Every single toy went back to its cozy home where it was safe from being bumped. The toys seemed happy in their neat spots. 

The bedroom was quiet now. Soft moonlight glowed through the curtains. Leo climbed into his warm bed and pulled the quilt up to his chin. Maya gave him a bedtime kiss and switched off the lamp. 

Leo looked around at the peaceful room. His books were resting. His toys were safe and sound. With a happy sigh, Leo closed his eyes and drifted off to sleep.
