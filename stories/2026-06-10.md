# Pip’s Brave Truth

**Date:** June 10, 2026  
**Theme:** being honest even when it is hard  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Pip the little bear loved warm June evenings. Tonight, he wanted to make a special surprise for his mama. He wanted to use her favorite blue cup to hold some sweet yellow flowers. Pip reached up high on the shelf, but his furry paw slipped. *Clink!* The cup fell into the sink and broke. 

Oh no! Pip’s tummy felt cold and tight. He did not mean to break it. What should he do now? 

Pip wanted to hide the broken cup. It is hard to say "I made a mistake" when you are scared. But Pip knew he would feel bad if he kept a secret. Mama always said that telling the truth is best. 

He took a deep breath. Pip walked out of the kitchen to find his mama. His heart was beating fast, but he knew he had to start talking. 

"Mama," Pip said softly. "I broke your blue cup. I wanted to put flowers in it. I am so sorry."

Mama Bear looked at Pip. She did not get angry. Instead, she knelt down and hugged him close. 

"Thank you for being honest, Pip," she said gently. "That took a lot of bravery. Now, let us see what we can do."

Together, they swept up the pieces. They could not fix the cup, but they saved the biggest blue piece. They placed it in the garden to help hold up a tiny, lovely flower of their own. 

The sky turned a soft, sleepy purple. Outside, the night birds began to sing their quiet lullaby. Pip felt so warm and happy inside. The heavy, cold feeling in his tummy was completely gone because he had told the truth. He climbed into his cozy bed and pulled up his blanket. He closed his eyes, ready to dream sweet summer dreams under the shining stars.
