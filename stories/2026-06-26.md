# Oliver’s Summer Sailboat

**Date:** June 26, 2026  
**Theme:** spending wisely vs. impulse buying  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Oliver was a small bear who lived in a cozy oak tree. Today was a warm summer day. He had three shiny silver coins of his own. Oliver wanted to buy a special wooden sailboat from the forest toy shop. It would make him so happy to sail it on the blue pond all summer long. 

Oliver decided to walk down the forest path. Along the way, he saw a big, bright tent filled with toys. 

"Come in!" called a friendly fox. "I have shiny green whistles and sweet honey treats!"

Oliver looked out at the toys. He could buy a whistle right now. Oliver thought, *I may want this whistle today, but the sailboat is what I truly love.* He knew he had to choose. It is better to wait for something special than to spend your coins on the first thing you see. 

"No, thank you," Oliver said. "I will keep my coins." He felt proud as he walked up the grassy hill. 

At the top of the hill, Oliver saw the shopkeeper open the wooden doors to the toy shop. Oliver put his three shiny coins on the counter. He got to take home his very own wooden sailboat. It was even more beautiful than he had dreamed! He could even write his name on the side of the boat with a green crayon. Oliver knew he had made the right choice. 

Now, the sun began to set, painting the sky in soft shades of pink and purple. The warm summer stars began to blink. It was time to start to sleep. Oliver set his lovely sailboat right next to his cozy bed. He pulled his soft blanket up to his chin. He closed his eyes, ready to dream of sailing on the quiet water. Waiting had been hard, but his heart was full and happy. 

Goodnight, little bear. Goodnight, little stars.
