# The Golden Chalk

**Date:** October 28, 2026  
**Theme:** sharing with friends  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

On a crisp October day, Oliver the little mouse sat on a smooth, flat stone. Autumn leaves danced in the breeze all around him. In his paws, he held a new box of colorful forest chalk. Oliver wanted to write a quiet story on the rock before the night arrived. 

Suddenly, Pip the squirrel hopped over with a cheerful wave. "Can I come play?" Pip asked kindly. "I would like to draw a sleepy autumn moon. Do you have a yellow chalk?"

Oliver looked down at his box. He only had one yellow stick, bright and perfect. Oliver paused, wondering what to do. What if Pip wore it all away? Part of him wanted to say no, but then he looked at his friend’s hopeful face. A pretty picture was nice, but having a friend was even better. 

"Here," Oliver said with a warm smile. "You can use it. We can both share the box."

Pip beamed and tapped his fluffy tail. "Thank you, Oliver! We can go on an adventure across the stone together."

Together, the two friends worked side by side until the shadows grew long. Oliver drew gentle purple hills, and Pip used the bright yellow to make tiny, glowing stars. As dry oak leaves tumbled around their paws, Oliver felt a cozy warmth inside his chest. Sharing his chalk did not take away his fun; it gave them twice as much joy.

Soon, the bright sun began to go down behind the sleepy trees, painting the sky in soft shades of lavender. The cool evening breeze whispered that it was time to rest. 

Pip gently placed the yellow chalk back into Oliver's tin. "Today was wonderful," Pip whispered softly.

"It truly was," Oliver replied, letting out a little mouse yawn.

Both friends waved goodnight and scampered off toward their cozy homes. Oliver curled up tightly in his warm, leafy bed, feeling peaceful, happy, and ready for sweet dreams.
