# Bramble’s Bedtime Sparkle

**Date:** October 11, 2026  
**Theme:** brushing teeth and healthy habits  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The cool October stars were peeking through the trees, and it had been a long, happy day of playing in the autumn leaves. Bramble the bunny knew it was time to get ready for sleep. He rubbed his heavy eyes and let out a slow, cozy yawn.

"Time for our healthy habits," Mama Rabbit said with a kind smile. Bramble took a quiet walk down the hall to the warm bathroom. He liked this peaceful part of the evening. Standing by the sink on his little wooden stool, he picked up his bright blue toothbrush.

Mama placed a tiny dot of minty paste onto the bristles. "Remember," she whispered gently, "we do not brush too fast. Your teeth work hard all day crunching carrots, so we take our time to care for them."

Bramble nodded. First, he brushed his top teeth, swirling the bristles in gentle circles like little dancing bubbles. Next, he brushed the bottom row. He made sure to reach each tooth, front and back, all the way to the very corners. 

How long does a bunny brush? He brushed for two whole minutes, humming a sweet, sleepy tune until Mama tapped his shoulder with a grin. Bramble rinsed with cool water and smiled in the mirror to see his teeth shine bright. Taking care of his smile was a good habit that made him feel fresh and happy inside. 

With clean teeth and soft paws, Bramble padded back to his bedroom. He climbed under his warm, fluffy quilt. Mama smoothed his long ears and kissed his forehead. 

"Your body is resting, your smile is clean, and you will have lovely dreams tonight," she whispered. 

Mama switched off the lamp, leaving only the silver moonbeams on the floor. Bramble curled up tight, listened to the quiet night breeze, and drifted happily off to sleep.
