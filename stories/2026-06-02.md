# The Shining June Star

**Date:** June 02, 2026  
**Theme:** taking turns and being patient  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

*Once* upon a warm June night, two little rabbits named Pip and Pop sat by their cozy bedroom window. The summer air smelled like sweet forest flowers. They had a brand new, bright *yellow* star-toy that projected glowing shapes onto the ceiling. 

"Can I try it first?" asked Pop. *He* really wanted to see the big, round moon light up their room. 

Pip smiled. "What *do* you want to see first?" Pip asked. "Remember, *we* can take turns so both of us get a chance."

Pop nodded. "I *wish* to see the shining stars first," he said. 

"Okay," said Pip. "First, let us *wash* our paws *for* bed. Then we can use the toy."

They ran to the sink to clean up. After that, Pip *put* on his softest blue pajamas. Since Pop was using the toy first, Pip had to wait his turn. He did not mind waiting. Instead, he chose to *read* a tiny book about cozy forest friends. Waiting was very easy and pleasant when you had a happy book to look at. 

"My turn is done," Pop whispered a few minutes later. He set the *yellow* toy down on the rug. "Thank you for being so patient, Pip."

Pip smiled. *Now* it was his turn. Pip looked *into* the glowing light of the toy. He pressed the button and projected the shapes. He saw *these* beautiful, twinkling stars and a smiling moon on the ceiling. They looked like silver dust floating in the air. 

Pop did not mind waiting now, because his eyes were getting very heavy. He let out a big, soft yawn. 

Together, the two rabbits decided to *open* the window to let the cool night breeze inside. 

"It is time to *sleep*," Pip whispered, turning off the toy. 

They snuggled under their warm blankets, happy that they had shared so nicely. The real stars shone down on the two patient little rabbits as they drifted off to dreamland.
