# Leo’s Garden Friend

**Date:** May 13, 2026  
**Theme:** being kind to animals  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The sun was setting on the evening of May 13. Leo sat on his back porch and watched the sky turn from orange to a soft purple. He saw something small move near the old oak tree. It was a tiny toad with bumpy skin. Leo wanted to run fast to see it, but he stopped himself. He remembered that being kind to animals means being very gentle and quiet.

The little toad looked like it was looking for a snack. His skin was a bit dry from the warm day. Leo thought of a number of ways he could help his new friend. He did not want to be scary, so he moved slowly. Those little toads are very small, and everything looks big to them. 

Leo went inside to wash a small blue dish from the kitchen. He filled it with cool, clear water. He walked back to the grass in a very careful way. He made sure there was no loud noise to scare the toad away. He placed the dish near a leafy plant and stepped back.

The toad looked at the water. It hopped over and sat right on the edge. It looked like it was very happy to have a drink. Leo felt a warm glow in his heart. Our garden is a home for many tiny friends, and it is important to take care of them. 

He did not try to pick the toad up. He knew that some animals are happiest when we just watch them with love. They both sat quietly in the soft grass as the first stars began to shine. It was a wonderful start to a peaceful night. 

"Goodnight, little friend," Leo whispered. 

The toad hopped back into the safety of the tall green leaves. Being kind to his garden neighbor made Leo feel sleepy and calm. He went inside, tucked himself into his cozy blankets, and fell fast asleep.
