# The Starry Swing

**Date:** August 28, 2026  
**Theme:** taking turns and being patient  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Once upon a late summer evening, Mia the mouse went for a quiet walk through the sleepy meadow. The twilight breeze was soft and warm. Along the path, she met her friend Leo the hedgehog. They were both smiling because they found something wonderful: a tiny hammock woven from sweet green grass.

"I want to try it first!" Mia squeaked happily, hopping near the hammock.

"I want to rest there, too," Leo said with a little sigh.

There was only one hammock, and it had room for just one friend to sit. How could they share it without feeling sad? 

"What does a good friend do when there is only one cozy spot?" Leo asked gently.

Mia thought for a moment. "We can take turns! That will make us both happy."

Mia climbed into the hammock first. Leo gave her a slow, gentle push. Back and forth she swayed. While Leo waited, he found a smooth stick to write tiny star shapes in the soft earth. Being patient gave him time to watch the golden fireflies glow. That made waiting feel peaceful, not long at all.

After a few calm minutes, Mia opened her eyes. "Your turn now, Leo," she whispered.

Mia hopped down, and Leo curled into the cozy grass seat. Now it was Mia’s turn to wait. She hummed a quiet song and watched the silver moon rise above the tall trees. Taking turns felt nice and easy.

When Leo finished his turn, they were both ready for sleep. Taking turns had filled their evening with kindness and calm.

"Thank you for sharing," whispered Leo.

"Thank you for being patient," whispered Mia.

Together, they began the slow walk home under the starry sky. Mia curled into her soft nest, and Leo nestled into his leafy burrow. They closed their eyes, drifting off to sweet dreams in the quiet night.
