# The Tower of Blue Pebbles

**Date:** April 28, 2026  
**Theme:** apologizing and making things right  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The moon was high over the Whispering Woods on this warm April night. Sam the Squirrel was very busy. He wanted to keep all his favorite acorns in one safe pile. He began to run fast to the hollow of his favorite oak tree. But Sam was moving much too fast. He did not see what was right in front of him.

His friend, Bluey the Bird, had spent the whole day at work. Bluey wanted to find the prettiest blue pebbles in the creek. He had stacked them into a very tall, shiny tower near the roots of the tree. Suddenly, crash! Sam ran right into the pebbles. They were scattered all over the mossy ground.

"Oh no!" Bluey chirped sadly. He felt like crying because his hard work was gone.

Sam stopped. He felt a heavy feeling in his tummy. He wanted to run away and hide, but he knew he should try to make things right. Sam took a deep breath and looked at his friend.

"I am very sorry, Bluey," Sam said softly. "I was not looking where I was going. I did not mean to knock down your beautiful tower. It was an accident, but I am still sorry."

Bluey looked at Sam and saw that his friend really cared. "Thank you for saying that," Bluey said. "It makes my heart feel much better."

"Can we build it back together?" Sam asked.

Bluey nodded with a smile. The two friends began to work. They had to sit down on the soft grass to reach the tiny, round stones. Soon, the tower began to grow again. As they worked, they began to laugh. Fixing the tower was even more fun than building it alone.

When they were finished, the tower was even taller. All the blue pebbles were back in place. Sam felt happy because the heavy feeling in his tummy was gone. "You are my best friend," Sam whispered.

The stars began to twinkle. It was time to find a place to rest. The woods became very quiet. Bluey went to his nest, and Sam curled up in his leaves. They were both ready to sleep. Sam knew that saying sorry and helping a friend was the best way to end the day.
