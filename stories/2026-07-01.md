# Barnaby’s Purple Berry

**Date:** July 01, 2026  
**Theme:** trying new foods  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The sun began to set on a warm July evening. Barnaby the little bunny sat at the kitchen table. He was very hungry after a **long** **day** of hopping in the green meadow. 

His mother **made** a special summer supper. On **the** plate was sweet clover, which Barnaby loved. **But** next to it was a small, round, purple berry. Barnaby had never seen one before. He tucked his ears down.

"What is that?" Barnaby asked, pointing his little paw. 

"It is a huckleberry," his mother said with a warm smile. "It grew near the river. Try it, my sweet bunny. You **may** like it."

Barnaby shook his head. "I only like clover," he whispered. "Clover is better **than** anything else."

His mother nodded gently. "Trying a new food can seem scary. **But** taking just a small **part** can **make** it a fun adventure. Take a **look** at it first."

Barnaby did **look** at the tiny purple berry. The soft evening **light** shone through the window, making the berry glow like a little purple star. He **did** want to be a brave little bunny. 

"I will take one tiny bite," Barnaby said. 

He closed his eyes. **Then** he took a very small nibble. 

*Crunch. Squish.* It was sweet, cool, and a little bit tangy! 

"Oh!" Barnaby gasped, his ears wiggling with joy. "This is very yummy! It tastes like a summer breeze."

"I am so proud of you for trying," his mother said. "Now, let **us** finish our supper and **then** **go** to bed."

Barnaby ate his berry with a big smile. He felt so happy and proud of himself. 

Soon, the stars began to blink in the sky. Barnaby curled up in his soft, warm bed, feeling safe and full. He closed his sleepy eyes, dreaming of all the delicious new foods he would try tomorrow. Goodnight, brave little bunny.
