# Lily Bunny’s Cozy Nest

**Date:** May 22, 2026  
**Theme:** finishing what you start  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Once upon a time, in a cozy green garden, lived a little rabbit named Lily. The May air was warm, and the bright stars were starting to shine in the night sky. Lily wanted to build a special bedtime nest. She had gathered soft yellow straw and fluffy white wool, but she was getting very sleepy. 

"I am too tired to finish," Lily said, looking at her half-made nest. "I could just leave it this way and go to sleep." She wanted to close her eyes right now. But the nest was not done. It was still open to the cool night breeze.

Her mother hopped over with a sweet, gentle smile. "How does a half-made bed feel?" she asked Lily. Lily sighed. "It does not feel very warm." Mother bunny said, "If we work together, we can finish what you started. When a job is done, it brings a peaceful night." Lily knew her mother was right. She wanted her nest to be perfect. 

They both worked to tuck the yellow straw all the way around the sides. They lined the inside with the softest wool. It was a little bit of work, but Lily kept going. Soon, the nest was complete. It looked like a cozy, fluffy cloud. Lily let out a happy laugh. She felt so proud of her hard work. 

"Come and rest now," her mother whispered, wrapping Lily in a soft hug. Lily climbed inside her completed nest. It was the warmest bed in the forest. As she thought about her lovely day, her eyes grew heavy. She drifted off to sleep, feeling happy that she had finished what she started.
