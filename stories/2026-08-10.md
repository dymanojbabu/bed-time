# Penny's Warm Gift

**Date:** August 10, 2026  
**Theme:** giving to others who need help  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Once upon a time, deep in the quiet woods, lived a little squirrel named Penny. The sun was slowly sinking behind the hills, and a cold evening breeze blew through the tall green trees. Penny was very tired after a long day of playing. It was almost time for rest, but first she wanted to make her nest soft and warm.

Penny scampered near the big oak tree and looked around. Soon, she found a rich pile of fluffy dandelion fluff and soft green moss. 

"Now I can get ready for sleep," Penny said with a happy little sigh. She gathered all the soft fluff in her arms. Her cozy nest was going to be the warmest place in the whole forest.

Just as she turned to go home, Penny heard a tiny shivering sound. She looked down and saw a little bird named Robin sitting on a low branch. Robin was shivering in the cold breeze. 

"I have no warm nest to sleep in tonight," Robin said softly. "It is very chilly up here."

Penny looked at the soft moss she had found. She really wanted an extra cozy bed, but she knew her friend needed help. Penny smiled warmly at Robin.

"I can share with you," Penny said. "I have plenty of soft fluff for both of us!"

Penny climbed up the branch and helped Robin build a snug little nest. She tucked the soft fluff all around her friend to keep her warm. Robin snuggled down with a sweet, quiet chirp.

"Thank you, Penny," Robin whispered. "I feel so safe now."

Giving her cozy moss to Robin made Penny feel glowing and warm inside her own heart. She trotted back to her hollow tree, tucked herself into her own nest, and looked up at the glowing moon. The woods were quiet now. Both little friends closed their eyes, floating off into peaceful dreams.
