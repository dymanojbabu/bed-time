# Oliver's Shiny Buttons

**Date:** August 11, 2026  
**Theme:** spending wisely vs. impulse buying  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

One warm summer day, Oliver the little squirrel finished his daily work. He had helped sweep the dusty porch and pick sweet berries in the sun. To thank him, his mother gave him three shiny golden buttons. "You did a wonderful job," she said. "You may spend them at the forest fair now."

Oliver ran fast down the winding path, his heart light and happy. He heard the crickets sing sweet bedtime songs, and he heard his friends laugh by the calm stream. 

At the fair, Oliver saw some bright red whistles that made a loud, funny sound. "Oh!" Oliver thought. "I want to buy one right away!" He held his buttons tightly in his tiny paw.

Then, Oliver paused. He looked up at the twinkling stars in the evening sky. He thought about what he truly wanted most of all. He wanted a special star-patterned blanket to keep him warm on cold nights. If he spent his buttons on a noisy whistle today, he would have none left for his cozy blanket.

"Do you know what you would like?" asked Wise Old Owl from behind his wooden table.

"I want to tell you my plan," Oliver said softly. "The funny whistle looks neat, but I want to save my buttons for the warm star blanket instead."

Wise Old Owl gave a gentle smile. "That is a wise choice," Owl said. "I can write your name on the blanket tag so it is saved just for you. Waiting for special things brings us a very happy heart."

Oliver felt proud and comfortable inside. He put his shiny buttons back into his safe pocket. He realized that choosing wisely felt much better than buying something fast just because it was new. 

Oliver walked back home under the glowing moon, ready to tuck into bed, close his eyes, and dream sweet, peaceful dreams.
