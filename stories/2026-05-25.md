# Barnaby and the Starry Piggy Bank

**Date:** May 25, 2026  
**Theme:** saving money and piggy banks  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Barnaby was a small bear with a big, bright dream. He wanted to buy a beautiful book about the night sky. He had been wishing for it for a very long time. Barnaby had a shiny, blue piggy bank named Pip that sat by his bed. Pip was always ready to help him save.

"Every little coin helps," his mother said with a warm smile. Barnaby learned how to save the shiny coins he earned. When he helped tidy the garden or sweep the green kitchen floor, he got a coin. Instead of running off to buy sweet treats that would vanish quickly, he would put his coins into Pip. *Clink, clink!* What a happy, musical sound they would make! 

One quiet evening, Barnaby wanted to check on his progress. "Let us see how much we have," Barnaby said to Pip. He and his mother sat together on the soft, fluffy rug. They counted the shiny coins one by one. Barnaby could use his math skills to add them up. He knew it would take some time, but saving his money made him feel very proud. "Soon, we will get the book," his mother whispered. They could already picture their cozy bedtime story time under the stars.

Barnaby tucked Pip safely back on the wooden shelf. He climbed into his warm, cozy bed and pulled up his blankets. He did not need to buy the book tonight to feel happy. Just knowing his savings were growing safe and sound made him feel warm inside. He closed his heavy eyes and started to dream about the bright, twinkling stars he would soon read about. As the soft silver moon shone through the window, the little bear fell fast asleep.
