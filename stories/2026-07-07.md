# Barnaby's Brave Night

**Date:** July 07, 2026  
**Theme:** being brave when scared  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Barnaby the little bear was ready for sleep. Outside, the summer night wind blew. His bedroom felt a little cold tonight. He looked out the window. The tall trees were shaking their leaves in the dark. 

"I do not like the wind," Barnaby whispered to his toy dog. "My room feels scary today because the wind makes funny noises."

Just then, his mother came into the room. She had always been there to help him feel safe. 

"What is wrong, my sweet bear?" she asked. She sat on the bed and patted her soft lap. 

"I have a little fear," Barnaby said. "That one sound outside is very loud. It is scary."

His mother smiled and hugged him close. "Being scared is okay, Barnaby. But you can be brave, too. Being brave is part of growing up. It means trying your best, even when you feel a little worried."

She pointed to the window. "Look at those green leaves. These are just branches dancing in the wind. They are playing a game."

To help him feel happy, his mother began to sing a sweet, quiet song. The song was so silly that it made Barnaby laugh. His worries did not feel so big anymore. 

He took a deep, slow breath. He knew he could be brave. He did not need to fear the wind. 

"Thank you, Mom," Barnaby said. He climbed back under his warm covers. He hugged his toy dog tight. 

He closed his eyes and listened to the soft wind. It sounded like a gentle lullaby. With a happy heart, the brave little bear closed his eyes and went to sleep.
