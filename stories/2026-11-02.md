# A Cozy Spot for Pip

**Date:** November 02, 2026  
**Theme:** being kind to animals  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The sun was sinking low at the end of a chilly November day. Bright leaves were falling all around the garden. Maya stepped out into the yard in her warm sweater. It was almost time for bed, but she wanted to watch the sunset first.

*Rustle, rustle.* Maya stopped. What was making that sound down near the wooden fence?

She walked softly so her boots would not make a fast crunch. Maya peered in between the bushes. Two dark, shiny eyes looked back at her. It was a tiny hedgehog, curled into a tight, prickly ball. 

Maya smiled gently. She did know a lot about wild creatures. She knew that sudden movements could frighten them, so she did not try to pick him up. Instead, she took two steps back to give him room.

"Hello, little friend," she whispered. "Are you looking to find a warm place to sleep?"

The little hedgehog uncurled his nose. Sniff, sniff went his snout. He saw that the kind girl was keeping him safe. He waddled around a tree root, searching for shelter from the cool breeze.

Maya wanted to help. She gathered an armful of dry leaves, and then one more. She placed them down gently by the quiet hedge, building a cozy nest. "Look how soft this is," Maya said in a slow, peaceful voice.

The hedgehog crept toward the golden mound. He burrowed right in, wiggling deep under the blanket of leaves until only his nose poked out. Maya felt happy and warm inside. It felt wonderful to show kindness to a small animal in need.

High above, the first stars were beginning to twinkle. Maya waved a quiet goodnight to her friend and walked back into the house. As she tucked into her own soft blankets, she closed her eyes, happy to know that both she and the little hedgehog were safe, warm, and ready to dream.
