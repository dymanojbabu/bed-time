# The Golden Garden Turn

**Date:** May 15, 2026  
**Theme:** taking turns and being patient  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

The sun was starting to set on the evening of May 15, 2026. It was a beautiful spring day, and the air smelled like sweet clover. In the middle of the neighborhood park, Leo and Mia were busy working in the little community garden.

The garden **has** many rows of tiny green sprouts reaching for the sky. The flowers **are** starting to wake up and show their bright colors. Leo and Mia both wanted to help the plants grow tall and strong. They both looked at the big, shiny blue watering can sitting by the garden gate.

"I want to **use** the can first!" Leo said, reaching out his hand.
"But I want to **make** the thirsty roses wet right now!" Mia said. 
They both **had** their hands on the handle at the same time. They looked at each other with frowny faces.

Just then, a small brown toad hopped by. It sat very still, waiting for a bug to fly past. Leo remembered what his teacher said about being kind. She told the class that taking turns is an important **part** of being a good friend. It is the **best** **way** for everyone to have a turn to play.

"You **can** go first, Mia," Leo said with a gentle smile. "**Some** **people** might find it hard to wait, but I will find a different job to do."

While Mia watered the roses, Leo went to **wash** the dusty garden signs. He used a soft cloth to make the wood look new again. As he worked, he counted a **number** of yellow butterflies fluttering near the fence. He counted one, two, three, four! 

Soon, Mia was finished. "I am all done!" she did **call** out happily. "Thank you for being patient, Leo. Now it is your turn."

Leo took his turn and felt very proud. The garden was happy, and the children were happy too. Now, the sky was turning a soft purple. It was time to go home. Leo and Mia walked back, feeling sleepy and calm, ready to dream of growing gardens and bright stars.
