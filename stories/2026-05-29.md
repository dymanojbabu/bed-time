# Bella’s Bright Idea

**Date:** May 29, 2026  
**Theme:** spending wisely vs. impulse buying  
**Ages:** 6-7

---

Bella the little bear **had** three shiny silver coins in **her** soft pocket. **She** wanted to **buy** something special at the busy forest market. 

As Bella walked **by** the colorful booths, **each** toy looked so fun. She saw a little toy frog that could hop. "I want to buy that **now**!" she thought. But the frog was made of weak plastic. It would break fast. Bella stopped and took a deep, slow breath. "If I buy this hopping frog," she said to herself, "I will have **no** coins left for **my** dream lantern."

Bella looked at the toy, then at her coins. **They** felt cold in her hand. But she knew they could **make** her room feel happy if she used them wisely. **She** knew the lantern **has** a soft, glowing light that would keep her room feeling **warm** at night. 

So, Bella walked past the cheap toys. She went to the lantern shop. The wise old owl **made** beautiful lanterns. He showed **them** to her. One special lantern had tiny stars cut into the side. 

"I would like to buy this, please," Bella said. She gave her shiny coins to the owl. 

**Now**, Bella was so glad she **had** not spent her money on the first toy she saw. Saving for something special made her heart happy. 

That night, Bella lay in her soft bed. The star-lantern was lit, casting a **warm** glow on her wooden walls. Her room felt safe and quiet. **She** pulled her cozy blankets close. 

"My mind is happy, and my room is bright," Bella whispered. With **no** worries in her head, she closed her eyes. The stars danced on her ceiling. **They** seemed to sing a quiet lullaby, wishing her the sweetest, wisest dreams.
